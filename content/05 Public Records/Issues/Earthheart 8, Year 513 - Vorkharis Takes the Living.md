# Vorkharis Takes the Living

[[05 Public Records/Forever Port Gazette|Forever Port Gazette]] · [[01 World/Fali-City and New Anglia#New Anglia|New Anglia]]

**Late Edition · Earthheart 8, Sunglade, Year 513 · Price: Two Copper**

The Gazette reports that disappearances across New Anglia form a pattern linking coastal hiring yards, inland roads, covered wagons, and hidden shore landings. Missing people include carters, healers, quarry hands, smiths, sailors, and solitary travelers.

Witnesses described wagons riding too low for their declared cargo and chained people moved beneath canvas. Survivors said captors sorted prisoners by useful trades rather than by name. Hobgoblin quartermasters, soot-darkened vessels, unregistered launches, and a blackened token marked by a broken chain around an ember appear repeatedly in the reports.

The paper connects these routes to volcanic labor sites and the title **Ash Sovereign**, publicly associating the activity with **Vorkharis**. It also states that no lawful order bearing Vorkharis's name had been produced and that the exact camps, number of captives, ships, and cooperating officials remained unproven.

> “They did not count names. They counted hands.”

## Travel advisory

- Travel in company and leave a route and expected return time with someone you trust.
- Report unusually heavy covered wagons, hidden ventilation holes, hooded red lanterns, unregistered shore launches, and broken-chain-and-ember tokens.
- Do not attempt a rescue alone.

This article records the Gazette's published claims. It does not establish the private identity of every captor or the complete organization behind the disappearances.
