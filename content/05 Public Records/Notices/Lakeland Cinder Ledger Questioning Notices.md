# Lakeland Cinder Ledger Questioning Notices

[[05 Public Records/Public Records Index|Public Records]]

The crew encountered five notices in Lakeland City about the **Cinder Ledger** and enslaved people in the **Morning Strait**. The notices name:

- Jinx
- Trifoot
- Vesper
- Xavier Fontaine
- Ryskulla

The instruction is explicit:

> CRITICAL: THESE ARE NOT FUGITIVES DO NOT IMPRISON.

These notices request statements; they do not establish a bounty or an arrest warrant. Vesper's name is preserved because it appears on the notice, even though the crew knows Vesper is deceased. No notice for Yama is established.

The local enchanted board replaces removed copies. Nearby Jinx's anti-slavery advice directs questions to Xavier, and Red Light Carnival handbills advertise the evening show.

**Encountered:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 015 - Vesper's Farewell and Lakeland Arrival|Session 015]], **14 Harvestshade, Year 513**.
