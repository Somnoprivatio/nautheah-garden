# Current Public Warnings

[[05 Public Records/Public Records Index|Public Records]]

## Vorkharis

The Forever Port Gazette publicly associates Vorkharis, called the **Ash Sovereign**, with disappearances, forced labor, hobgoblin quartermasters, coastal transfers, and volcanic work sites in New Anglia. The connection is a serious published allegation, not a complete legal finding.

Travelers in New Anglia are advised to travel together, record employers and routes, and report suspicious wagons, launches, lantern signals, or broken-chain-and-ember marks. See [[05 Public Records/Issues/Earthheart 8, Year 513 - Vorkharis Takes the Living|Vorkharis Takes the Living]].

## Vortex of Mist

Mana storms and extreme magical fluctuations remain persistent travel hazards even after earlier commissioned problems in the region were resolved.

## Uninhabited Island

The island remains without known government or inhabitants. A completed survey commission does not make the wider island safe or fully explored.
