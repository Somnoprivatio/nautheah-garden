# Forever Port Gazette

[[05 Public Records/Public Records Index|Public Records]]

The **Forever Port Gazette** is a far-reaching newspaper circulated through Forever Port and carried along Nautheah's shipping routes. Its masthead promises *“An Honest Word in Every Tide.”* Individual issues mix coastal reporting, harbor warnings, testimony, and calls for evidence.

The Gazette is an in-world source. Its reporting is public and researchable, but it is not an omniscient narrator. Readers should distinguish witnessed facts, attributed testimony, editorial conclusions, and explicitly unproven claims.
