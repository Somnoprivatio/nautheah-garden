# Tobren “Tob” Vale

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]] · [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch|Tidemarch]] · [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch/Nine Bells Quay|Nine Bells Quay]]

Tobren “Tob” Vale is a halfling quay dockmaster and former rigger working along Tidemarch's Low Quays. He assigns private and overflow berths, checks tide clearance, signals incoming vessels, and coordinates mooring crews at Nine Bells Quay.

## Public record

- Quay dockmaster responsible for Nine Bells Quay.
- Former professional rigger with extensive knowledge of Tidemarch's pilings, tide windows, and harbor signals.
- Known for quick decisions, red-and-white signal flags, and a dry dockworker's humor.
