# Captain Maelin Veyr

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]] · [[03 Factions/Iron Authority|Iron Authority]] · [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Crew and Ship#Iron Calamity|Iron Calamity]]

Captain Maelin Veyr commands the **Iron Calamity**, a major naval vessel of the [[03 Factions/Iron Authority|Iron Authority]]. Her position places her among Forever Port's most visible maritime officers and gives her authority over naval patrols, inspections, and responses to serious threats at sea.

## Public record

- Captain of the Iron Calamity.
- Senior officer in the Iron Authority's naval service.
- Operates under the public command of [[02 People/Vorkesh Sorn|Vorkesh Sorn]].

Her personal history, private loyalties, and current assignments beyond the Iron Calamity are not established in the public record.
