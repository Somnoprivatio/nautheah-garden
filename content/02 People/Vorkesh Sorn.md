# Vorkesh Sorn

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]] · [[03 Factions/Iron Authority|Iron Authority]] · [[01 World/Forever Port|Forever Port]] · [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Missions and Locations|Campaign Missions and Locations]]

**Vorkesh Sorn** is the head of the [[03 Factions/Iron Authority|Iron Authority]], Forever Port's naval military. He is the senior public authority responsible for the city's naval defense, harbor patrols, and maritime enforcement.

## Public relevance

The Iron Authority's position places Sorn at the center of Forever Port's response to threats at sea, including the wider investigation into disappearances, forced labor, and vessels associated with the **Ash Sovereign** reports. The public record does not establish Sorn's personal involvement in any alleged crime or reveal details beyond his official command.
