# Aeris Moonshadow

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]] · [[03 Factions/Circle of the Moon|Circle of the Moon]] · [[03 Factions/Fali-City Adventuring Academy|Fali-City Adventuring Academy]] · [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Missions and Locations|Campaign Missions and Locations]]

Aeris Moonshadow is a druid associated with the **Circle of the Moon**. Public family records identify him as the son of **Sylvaris Moonshadow** and grandson of **Elder Sylvanos**.

## Current campaign relevance

Wren Treadwater identified Aeris as a possible contact for questions about **Selanora's Seeds**, the Black Currents, and the Moonlit Undertow. She said a Forever Port outpost could route a message, while the **Fali-City Adventuring Academy** might offer the party a better chance of reaching him.

The party's public record treats Aeris as a potential contact and source of lunar and wilderness knowledge. A complete account of his present location, duties, and Circle authority has not been published.
