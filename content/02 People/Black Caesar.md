# Black Caesar

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]] · [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Cinder Ledger Briefing|Cinder Ledger Briefing]] · [[01 World/Forever Port|Forever Port]]

Black Caesar is the captain's name attached to the **Cinder Ledger**, a vessel under investigation in the current Northern Current Carnies campaign. The name is relevant to the party's inquiry into ships and routes associated with the published Vorkharis reports.

## Publicly relevant information

- Caesar is associated with the Cinder Ledger's command.
- The Cinder Ledger is an active investigative lead, not a resolved public case.
- The **Forever Port Gazette** reports disappearances, forced labor, coastal transfers, and volcanic work sites associated with the title **Ash Sovereign**. Those reports do not independently prove Caesar's personal orders or allegiance.

## Not established in the public record

Caesar's appearance, origins, former affiliations, crew complement, current location, and final fate have not been published as public facts. No completed interception or trial is recorded here.
