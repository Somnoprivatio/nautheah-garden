# Malithar Windrider

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]] · [[03 Factions/Fali-City Adventuring Academy|Fali-City Adventuring Academy]] · [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Missions and Locations|Campaign Missions and Locations]]

**Malithar Windrider** is a wizard and an official mentor at the **Fali-City Adventuring Academy**. He serves as a mentor to **Aeris Moonshadow and Aeris's adventuring party**.

His academy role makes him a potential point of contact for adventurers seeking instruction, introductions, or a route toward Circle of the Moon knowledge. No broader biography or political office has been established in the public record.
