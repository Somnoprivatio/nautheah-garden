# Civic and World Figures

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

## Leroy Jenk

Leroy Jenk is a captain of Elpa's main port guard. **Leroy Jenk** is the casual form of his name; **Leroy of House Jenk** is his formal noble style. He is large, heavily armored, and carries the Elpa Port Guard insignia of waves and a castle above and below the water, with an osprey shoulder emblem.

Leroy publicly belongs to [[03 Factions/Houses Jenk and Shellstitch#House Jenk|House Jenk]]. He has claimed past service aboard **Queen Anne's Revenge**, but the precise nature of that service is not established in the public record.

## Elder Sylvanos

Elder Sylvanos is a known elder associated with the [[03 Factions/Circle of the Moon|Circle of the Moon]] tradition. Fine details of the Circle's authority, abilities, and current council are not yet established as public canon.

## Sylvaris Moonshadow

Sylvaris Moonshadow is publicly connected to Elder Sylvanos and the Moonshadow family line. The public record keeps the family and druidic association while withholding character-private history and unconfirmed offices.

## Captain Maelin Veyr

Captain Maelin Veyr commands the [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Crew and Ship#Iron Calamity|Iron Calamity]], a major vessel of the [[03 Factions/Iron Authority|Iron Authority]]. She is a visible maritime officer of Forever Port and operates under the public command of [[02 People/Vorkesh Sorn|Vorkesh Sorn]]. Her private history and current assignments are not part of the public record.

## Lieutenant Kaharvesh

Lieutenant Kaharvesh is a known officer aboard the [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Crew and Ship#Iron Calamity|Iron Calamity]], serving under Captain Maelin Veyr. He is publicly associated with patrol, inspection, and naval-security duties around Forever Port; personal history and current orders remain undisclosed.

## Israel Hands

Israel Hands is a sailor associated with Elpa's port guard and the maritime tradition surrounding the [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Crew and Ship#Queen Anne's Revenge|Queen Anne's Revenge]]. He is known in port circles as an experienced seaman and keeper of old sea songs. His precise service history and loyalties are not established in the public record.
