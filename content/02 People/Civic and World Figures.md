# Civic and World Figures

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

## Leroy Jenk

[[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Leroy Jenk|Leroy Jenk]] is an Elpa port-guard captain and member of House Jenk.

## Elder Sylvanos

[[02 People/World/World Leaders/Elder Sylvanos|Elder Sylvanos]] is associated with the Circle of the Moon and is Aeris's grandfather.

## Sylvaris Moonshadow

[[02 People/World/World Leaders/Sylvaris Moonshadow|Sylvaris Moonshadow]] is Aeris's father and part of the known Moonshadow family connection.

## Captain Maelin Veyr

[[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Captain Maelin Veyr|Captain Maelin Veyr]] commands the Iron Calamity.

## Lieutenant Kaharvesh

[[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Lieutenant Kaharvesh|Lieutenant Kaharvesh]] serves aboard the Iron Calamity and conducted the questioning at sea.

## Israel Hands

[[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Israel Hands|Israel Hands]] is the sailor who taught the crew the shanty and rejoined Queen Anne's Revenge.
