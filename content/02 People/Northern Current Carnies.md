# Northern Current Carnies

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]] · [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

The Northern Current Carnies are the five active player characters aboard the [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Crew and Ship#Drowning Promise|Drowning Promise]]. This roster contains public and party-shared information only.

## Trifoot Everfold

A male Tabaxi sailor from Morning Dew. Trifoot became captain during the mana-storm mutiny on Stormpeak 22, Year 513. He wears a distinctive sea captain's tricorn and travels with a pseudodragon named Luza. The tricorn's deeper nature is not public.

## Xavier

One of the Drowning Promise's longest-serving crew members and an important officer under Trifoot. His ancestry, class, and formal shipboard office have not been publicly recorded.

## Jinx

A masked carnival performer and current crew member. Jinx deals in tricks, performance, and misdirection. Private history and the nature of the mask are omitted.

## Ryskulla

A D&D player character portrayed by Dregn Ryder and a current member of the Drowning Promise's crew. Proposed backstory details are not treated as public history unless separately confirmed.

## Vesper

An aasimar carnival strong-person and experienced shipwright who joined in Elpa. Vesper knowingly accepted the crew's piracy and stated opposition to slavery before remaining aboard.
