# Northern Current Carnies

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

## Trifoot Everfold

[[02 People/Campaign Specific/PC/Trifoot Everfold|Trifoot]] is the Tabaxi captain of the Drowning Promise, accompanied by Luza.

## Xavier

[[02 People/Campaign Specific/PC/Xavier Fontaine|Xavier Fontaine]] is a senior officer, scholar and spellcaster.

## Jinx

[[02 People/Campaign Specific/PC/Jinx|Jinx]] is the masked carnival performer whose advice has circulated in Lakeland.

## Yama

[[02 People/Campaign Specific/PC/Yama|Yama]] is the ally who killed Abe and now travels aboard; his formal crew place is open.

## Ryskulla

[[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Ryskulla|Ryskulla]] left on 2 Harvestshade to find her family and intends to send word.

## Vesper

[[02 People/Campaign Specific/PC/Vesper|Vesper]] was the crew's aasimar shipwright and strong-person. They died in the laboratory confrontation and were later buried at sea.
