# Sylvaris Moonshadow

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Sylvaris is associated with the Moonshadow family and the [[03 Factions/Orders and Schools/Circle of the Moon|Circle of the Moon]]. He is Aeris Moonshadow's father, and Aeris is Elder Sylvanos's grandson.

Wren identified that family connection when recommending Aeris as a contact. A personal meeting with Sylvaris has not been recorded.
