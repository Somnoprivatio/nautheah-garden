# Elder Sylvanos

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Elder Sylvanos is a known elder associated with the [[03 Factions/Orders and Schools/Circle of the Moon|Circle of the Moon]]. He is Aeris Moonshadow's grandfather and part of the established Moonshadow family connection.

The crew discussed him as a possible source of seed knowledge, then reached Aeris through the academy. A meeting with Sylvanos has not occurred in the shared record.
