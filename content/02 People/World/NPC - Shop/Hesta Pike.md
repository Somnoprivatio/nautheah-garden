# Hesta Pike

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Hesta is the gnome innkeeper and restaurateur who owns [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch/The Nine Bells|The Nine Bells]], attached to Nine Bells Quay in Tidemarch. She feeds yard crews, keeps rooms near the harbor and uses the nine salvaged bells to announce tides, arrivals and storm warnings.

She told the visiting crew that skilled maritime workers and other useful tradespeople had disappeared in repeated waves while the authorities did little. The unfinished work and understaffed yards around town gave the party visible reasons to take the report seriously.
