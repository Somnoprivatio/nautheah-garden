# Keelwhisper

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Keelwhisper is a male Tabaxi pirate, known as the **Whisper beneath the Keel**, who owns and operates [[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Regions/Forever Port/Talontinous Delight|Talontinous Delight]] in Forever Port.

He accepted Maclear's delayed cargo, arranged its unloading and knew Korvash as "old Corvie." In the crew's meeting, he explained the Cinder Ledger handoff window and identified the Ashen Tithe as a mercenary organization taking living people for its employer.

[[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - PC Backstory/Wren Treadwater|Wren]] works under his protection. She described his provision of lodging, tools, pay and magical instruction; the party saw the workshop and concealed contact operation at the tavern.
