# Veyra Shellstitch

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Veyra is the six-armed Simic Hybrid tailor of [[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Regions/Elpa/Shops/The Second Skin|The Second Skin]] in Elpa. Coral grows through her hair. Her business fits clients for both surface and underwater life, and she is a prominent member of [[03 Factions/Noble Houses/House Shellstitch|House Shellstitch]].

Jinx put her to sleep before Trifoot stole Captain's Pride armor. The shop was subsequently ransacked by a still-unidentified party. Elpa authorities reported that Veyra had been taken into underwater caverns and later found alive. She recovered and completed Jinx's fine clothing from personal stock, but the missing stock prevented Ryskulla's armor commission.

The later theft's culprit and motive remain unknown.
