# Caldris Vane

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Caldris is the Triton swordsmith who runs [[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Regions/Elpa/Shops/Keelbreaker Blades|Keelbreaker Blades]] in Elpa's above-water city. His trade includes maritime blades, enchantment commissions and rear pressure-testing tanks for underwater work.

Jinx acquired the polished wooden Inverse Rapier of Verdict from him. Ryskulla commissioned a light-hammer enchantment, and Trifoot commissioned a scimitar with enhancement and warning magic. Both returned to collect their weapons before leaving Elpa.

**Shared encounters:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 002 - Elpa Heist, Enchantments, and a Curse|Session 002]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 003 - A Charlatan's Bargain and Forever Port|Session 003]].
