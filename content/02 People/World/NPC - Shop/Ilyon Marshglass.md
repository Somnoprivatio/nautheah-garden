# Ilyon Marshglass

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Ilyon "Ily" Marshglass is the sea elf reagent broker and alchemical supplier at [[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Regions/Elpa/Shops/The Brined Crucible|The Brined Crucible]] in Elpa, using he/they pronouns.

The party bought spell components and component pouches there. Ilyon also gave Jinx a sigil-covered stone intended for Ryskulla. His comment that their auras moved between lawful and unlawful while seeming mostly good was his perception, rather than a judgment imposed on the crew.

**Shared encounter:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 002 - Elpa Heist, Enchantments, and a Curse|Session 002]].
