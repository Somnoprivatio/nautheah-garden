# Nimble

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Nimble is the young gnome described in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Galdric Tombstone Materials/Galdric Tombstone — Journal and History of the Swaying Peaks Brotherhood|Galdric Tombstone's recovered journal]]. The journal places him with a traveling band of drow guides and liberators encountered by Nocte Equi Trojani after a purple-worm battle.

Galdric described his alertness to small signs of danger. This is a historical account in the recovered writings, rather than a meeting with the Northern Current Carnies.
