# Hesta Pike

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]] · [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch|Tidemarch]] · [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch/The Nine Bells|The Nine Bells]]

Hesta Pike is the gnome innkeeper and restaurateur who owns **The Nine Bells**, a dockside restaurant, tavern, and inn attached to Nine Bells Quay in Tidemarch.

She is publicly known for feeding yard crews at shift change, keeping clean rooms near the harbor, and using the nine salvaged bells above the inn to announce tides, arrivals, and storm warnings.
