# Blackbeard

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Blackbeard's name is associated with the legendary [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Ships/Queen Anne's Revenge|Queen Anne's Revenge]]. Leroy claimed to have been his first mate, and Black Caesar reported having sailed with his pirates before a split over slavery.

The crew has speculated that Leroy may be Blackbeard. That is a party theory; Blackbeard's identity, exact relationship to Leroy and present fate have not been established in the shared account.

**Shared record:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 002 - Elpa Heist, Enchantments, and a Curse|Session 002]], [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 008 - Destruction of the Cinder Ledger|Session 008]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 009 - The Queen Anne's Revenge Rises|Session 009]].
