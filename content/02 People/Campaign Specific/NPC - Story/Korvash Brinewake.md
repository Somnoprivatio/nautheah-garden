# Korvash Brinewake

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Korvash is an older Simic sailor and experienced deckhand aboard the [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Ships/Drowning Promise|Drowning Promise]]. He uses a hammock in the mainmast crow's nest and has a longstanding acquaintance with [[02 People/World/NPC - Shop/Keelwhisper|Keelwhisper]], who calls him **old Corvie**.

An umber hulk badly wounded his abdomen during the Underdark ambush. Brother Oren treated him, and he recovered during the voyage. He later corrected the ship's course after an inexperienced helmsman scraped a coral embankment, accepted a fine fishing net for crew use and spoke the rites at Vesper's burial at sea.
