# Tobren "Tob" Vale

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Tobren is the halfling quay dockmaster and former rigger responsible for [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch/Nine Bells Quay|Nine Bells Quay]] in Tidemarch. He assigns private and overflow berths, checks tide clearance, signals incoming vessels and coordinates mooring crews.

He is Vesper's childhood friend. When the Drowning Promise arrived, he directed it away from the main commercial berths and into Nine Bells Quay's relative privacy.
