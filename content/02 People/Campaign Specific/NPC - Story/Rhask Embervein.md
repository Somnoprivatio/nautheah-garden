# Rhask Embervein

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Rhask introduced himself as a **self-appointed Tidemarch security officer** and thanked the crew for handling the local "beast problem" after the laboratory battle. The waiting group supplied regional port and trade charts, fishing charts, a Whoasis smuggling-caravan route map and a fine fishing net.

This is Rhask's stated role. The encounter did not disclose a larger organization behind the group.
