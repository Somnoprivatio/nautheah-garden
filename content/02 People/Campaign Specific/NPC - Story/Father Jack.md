# Father Jack

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Father Jack is Vesper's father and lived in the family's neglected Tidemarch home. When Vesper returned, the crew found him drinking in a distressed state. After Vesper's uncontrolled transformation, they helped restore calm; Vesper healed Jack's forehead and carried him inside.

The later laboratory discovery placed him alive but altered in a vat. Abe identified himself as Jack's father and Vesper's paternal grandfather. During the battle, Abe transformed Jack into a flesh golem, and Trifoot killed him.

An addressed paper taken at the house was unread at the end of the homecoming. Its contents are not part of the shared account.
