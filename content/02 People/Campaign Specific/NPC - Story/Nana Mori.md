# Nana Mori

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Nana is a receptionist and official contact at the [[03 Factions/Orders and Schools/Fali-City Adventuring Academy|Fali-City Adventuring Academy]]. She handles messages, appointments and academy routing, including the crew's path toward Aeris Moonshadow.
