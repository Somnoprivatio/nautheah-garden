# Black Caesar

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Black Caesar commanded the [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Ships/Cinder Ledger|Cinder Ledger]], the slaving vessel boarded by the party on 13 Sunglade, Year 513. He was captured alive, disarmed and manacled. One hundred living captives were found aboard.

Caesar reportedly said he had sailed with Blackbeard's pirates, split from them over the slave trade and scuttled his earlier ship before joining the Cinder Ledger. This remains his attributed account.

At Leroy's request, the party took Caesar far out to sea. He was transferred to the risen Queen Anne's Revenge, chained to its mast and drowned beneath the ocean. The crew cast his cleaned bones into the water before returning the party to the surface. **Caesar is dead.** His encoded book and other recovered records remain part of the crew's evidence.
