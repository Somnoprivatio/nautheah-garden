# Vorkesh Sorn

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Vorkesh Sorn is the public head of Forever Port's [[03 Factions/Authorities/Iron Authority|Iron Authority]] naval military. His official responsibilities include naval defense, harbor patrols and maritime enforcement.

## What the crew has uncovered

During the Forever Port interrogation, an oath-bound mercenary died while attempting to reveal Sorn's name. The same investigation established the Ashen Tithe capture cell, the wagon's living captives and a Cinder Ledger handoff. The party also recovered official port-protection letters from the vessel.

These discoveries make Sorn a serious investigative lead. They do not by themselves reveal the entire command chain or establish that every Iron Authority officer shares the same knowledge or involvement.
