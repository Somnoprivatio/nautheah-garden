# Lieutenant Kaharvesh

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Kaharvesh is an officer aboard the [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Ships/Iron Calamity|Iron Calamity]], serving under Captain Maelin Veyr in the Iron Authority.

He questioned the Drowning Promise after the Cinder Ledger explosion, accepted the crew's cover story and issued a small passage/clearance ring. He told them to say the Iron Calamity had already questioned and cleared them if another ship challenged them. The ring was later identified as a [[04 Lore/Recovered Objects/Iron Calamity Sending Ring|Sending device]] that contacts the Iron Calamity directly.

**Shared encounter:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 008 - Destruction of the Cinder Ledger|Session 008]]; ring identification in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 009 - The Queen Anne's Revenge Rises|Session 009]].
