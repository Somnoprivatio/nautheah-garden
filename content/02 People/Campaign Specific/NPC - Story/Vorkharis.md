# Vorkharis

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

**Vorkharis**, the **Ash Sovereign**, is publicly described as a volcanic dragon and a major threat in the Forever Port Gazette.

## Public reports

The Gazette associates his title with disappearances in New Anglia, covered wagons and shore launches carrying captives, hobgoblin quartermasters, soot-darkened vessels and volcanic work sites. The locations and complete command structure remain unconfirmed in the crew's evidence.

## What the party has learned

The broken-chain-and-ember mark on the Ashen Tithe wagon was identified as the employer's mark for that job. Four people were rescued from the wagon, and the party later found one hundred captives aboard the Cinder Ledger and destroyed the ship.

Trifoot warned the group that Vorkharis knew about them and had offered a future challenge. The group has not received the full contents of that private encounter.
