# Captain Maelin Veyr

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Maelin commands the [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Ships/Iron Calamity|Iron Calamity]] in the [[03 Factions/Authorities/Iron Authority|Iron Authority]], serving under the public command of Vorkesh Sorn. She is an imposing half-giant woman, **7 ft 8 in tall and 250 lb**, with a **six-barrel rifle**.

[[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Lieutenant Kaharvesh|Lieutenant Kaharvesh]] is a known officer aboard her vessel. The crew first learned her name during its Iron Calamity contact after the Cinder Ledger's destruction. Her public role concerns naval patrols, inspections and maritime security.

The shared campaign record reaches Lakeland's request for a Cinder Ledger statement. Her personal loyalties and any official conclusion about the party's allegations have not been established as shared knowledge.

**Public and campaign reference:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 008 - Destruction of the Cinder Ledger|Session 008]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 015 - Vesper's Farewell and Lakeland Arrival|Session 015]].
