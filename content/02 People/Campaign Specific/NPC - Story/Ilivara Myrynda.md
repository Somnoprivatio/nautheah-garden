# Ilivara Myrynda

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Ilivara Myrynda bears the title **High Priestess Grace**. She serves [[04 Lore/Deities/Active/Selune|Selune]], Nautheah's lesser moon goddess associated with drow worship, at a [[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Regions/Forever Port/Drow Moon Temple|moon temple in Forever Port]].

Her temple received Mara Fenlock after the wagon rescue and provided care for dehydration and confinement. The shared encounter establishes her title, faith and aid to the rescued captive.

**Shared encounter:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 005 - Captive Wagon and Cinder Ledger|Session 005]].
