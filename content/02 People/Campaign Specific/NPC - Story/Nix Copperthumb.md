# Nix Copperthumb

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Nix is a goblin tidewright and hull-patch crafter from [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch|Tidemarch]]. He was one of the two skilled captives held separately aboard the Cinder Ledger. After the rescue he accepted work aboard the Drowning Promise.

He recognized Vesper's connection to Tidemarch without immediately recognizing Vesper themselves. After the crew returned to the town, he voluntarily left the ship to seek work at [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch/Gond's Nail|Gond's Nail]].
