# Captain Maclear

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Maclear was the Drowning Promise's captain before the mana-storm mutiny. Trifoot cut his tether and he fell from the ship into the sea; the crew accepted Trifoot as captain.

The party recovered funds, charts, unusual equipment and the book and map of [[04 Lore/Discoveries/Selanora's Seeds|Selanora's Seeds]] from his quarters. His delayed cargo was later accepted and unloaded by Keelwhisper at Forever Port. Its contents have not been established in the shared account.

**Shared record:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 001 - The Storm Mutiny|Session 001]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 003 - A Charlatan's Bargain and Forever Port|Session 003]].
