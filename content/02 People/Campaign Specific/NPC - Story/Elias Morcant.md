# Elias Morcant

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Elias, initially calling himself **Eli**, led the masked cart ambush against Jinx and Vesper in Forever Port. Vesper subdued him, and he survived for questioning.

He identified himself as a New Anglia native and an operative of the [[03 Factions/Networks/Ashen Tithe|Ashen Tithe]]. The cell had a quota of six living captives: four were hidden in the wagon, and Jinx and Vesper were intended to complete the quota. His answers were truthful but incomplete.

The wagon's concealed chambers, tide card and delivery equipment pointed toward the Cinder Ledger. Elias is the capture-cell leader, rather than that vessel's captain. His later disposition has not been recorded in the shared account.
