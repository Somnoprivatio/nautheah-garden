# Mara Fenlock

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Mara Fenlock was one of the four living captives rescued from the Ashen Tithe wagon in Forever Port. They were a human itinerant healer found conscious but severely dehydrated.

The party took Mara to the [[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Regions/Forever Port/Drow Moon Temple|Drow moon temple]], where she received care for dehydration and confinement. Vesper also provided healing.

**Shared encounter:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 005 - Captive Wagon and Cinder Ledger|Session 005]].

They were released into Forever Port after the rescue. Their later destination has not been reported.
