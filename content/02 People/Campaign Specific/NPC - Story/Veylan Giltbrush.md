# Veylan Giltbrush

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Veylan is the two-tailed vulpin proprietor and self-proclaimed impresario of the [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch/Old Smokehouse|Old Smokehouse]] in Tidemarch. He prefers **Master Giltbrush**, calls people "my dear" and remembers unpaid debts.

He hosted Jinx's flaming-knife and circus-ball performance, agreeing to a ten-percent venue arrangement. Before the crew departed Tidemarch, he publicly praised Jinx's talent.

**Shared record:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 012 - Tidemarch Homecoming|Session 012]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 015 - Vesper's Farewell and Lakeland Arrival|Session 015]].
