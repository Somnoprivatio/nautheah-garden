# Leroy Jenk

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Leroy Jenk, formally **Leroy of House Jenk**, is a captain in Elpa's main port guard and a member of [[03 Factions/Noble Houses/House Jenk|House Jenk]]. He is heavily armored, carries waves-and-castle port-guard insignia and wears an osprey shoulder emblem. His office is in the courthouse.

His letter provided the Drowning Promise's reversible merchant-and-pirate flag and claimed that he had served as Blackbeard's first mate aboard Queen Anne's Revenge. The crew has not established his exact former role or confirmed the theory that he is Blackbeard.

The armor-theft dispute became a **300-platinum principal plus one favor**. Trifoot paid the principal in Elpa and completed the favor by taking Black Caesar out to sea for Umberlee's claim. Both obligations are settled.
