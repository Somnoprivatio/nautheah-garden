# Malithar Windrider

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Malithar is a wizard and official mentor at the [[03 Factions/Orders and Schools/Fali-City Adventuring Academy|Fali-City Adventuring Academy]]. He mentors Aeris Moonshadow and Aeris's adventuring party.

The Northern Current Carnies met the academy's contacts during their effort to reach the Circle of the Moon. The academy arranged their return to the Drowning Promise by teleportation.

**Shared encounter:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 006 - Infiltrating the Cinder Ledger|Session 006]].
