# Ryskulla

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Ryskulla is a former adventuring member of the Drowning Promise's crew. She took part in the storm mutiny, the Elpa visit, the Cinder Ledger rescue and the Underdark passage, and showed an aptitude for examining unusual magic.

She left on the morning of **2 Harvestshade, Year 513**, while the party pursued Abe. Her message explained that she was searching for her family, did not want the ship to wait for a trail she could not yet name, and intended to contact the crew when she knew more. Her route and family's location are unknown.

The party discovered her message in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 015 - Vesper's Farewell and Lakeland Arrival|Session 015]]. She is also named on Lakeland's Cinder Ledger questioning notices, which explicitly say the named crew are not fugitives and must not be imprisoned.
