# Israel Hands

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Israel is an experienced sailor associated with Elpa's port guard and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Ships/Queen Anne's Revenge|Queen Anne's Revenge]]. Leroy sent him with the Drowning Promise when the crew took Black Caesar out to sea.

He taught the crew sea shanties. When the Revenge rose from the depths in response to its song, Israel recognized it and rejoined its crew. The party followed the ship below the sea to witness Caesar's execution and was returned to the surface afterward. Before departing, Israel warned them not to accept an offer of eternal sailing from Umberlee.

**Shared encounter:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 009 - The Queen Anne's Revenge Rises|Session 009]].
