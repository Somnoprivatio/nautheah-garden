# Madruk Inkmaul

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Madruk was the Cinder Ledger's first mate and arcane scribe. He detected the boarding party entering through the captain's window but was overcome during the attack. He did not survive the boarding.

The party recovered his spellbook, component pouch, maul and the Cinder Ledger's activity ledger. Xavier later discovered that at least one spellbook page was missing. Questioning and a search of the Drowning Promise did not find it or identify the person responsible.
