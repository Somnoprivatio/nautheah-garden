# Pell Underbough

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Pell Underbough was one of the four living captives rescued from the Ashen Tithe wagon in Forever Port. They were a halfling courier found unconscious but stable.

The rescue established their identity and condition; later whereabouts have not been reported.

**Shared encounter:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 005 - Captive Wagon and Cinder Ledger|Session 005]].

They were released into Forever Port after the rescue. Their later destination has not been reported.
