# Galdric Tombstone

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Galdric was a dwarf monk and librarian of the [[03 Factions/Orders and Schools/Swaying Peaks Brotherhood|Swaying Peaks Brotherhood]]. Vesper brought him aboard from an Underdark shore, badly wounded and carrying disease, venom and poison that resisted the available healing. Brother Oren attributed his injuries to a drow attack.

Knowing he would die, Galdric asked for ale and a final revelry in honor of [[04 Lore/Deities/Active/Hanseath|Hanseath]]. The crew shared the celebration, and he died among them.

His pack contained a blessed, glowing history tome, his journal, feast records and underground brewing notes. [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Galdric Tombstone Materials/Galdric Tombstone — Journal and History of the Swaying Peaks Brotherhood|His recovered journal]] describes traveling with Nocte Equi Trojani, a purple worm and its brood, Nimble and drow liberators, and the clearing of Mount Neveah's mists. Those older events are preserved as journal testimony and Brotherhood tradition.
