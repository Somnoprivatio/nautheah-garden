# Aeris Moonshadow

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Aeris is a druid associated with the [[03 Factions/Orders and Schools/Circle of the Moon|Circle of the Moon]], son of [[02 People/World/World Leaders/Sylvaris Moonshadow|Sylvaris Moonshadow]] and grandson of [[02 People/World/World Leaders/Elder Sylvanos|Elder Sylvanos]].

Wren recommended him as a contact for the seed investigation. The crew contacted him through the [[03 Factions/Orders and Schools/Fali-City Adventuring Academy|Fali-City Adventuring Academy]] and met him in Forever Port. He verified their account and offered conditional access to the Circle, warning against hostility and misuse of the seeds. [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Malithar Windrider|Malithar Windrider]] is his academy mentor.
