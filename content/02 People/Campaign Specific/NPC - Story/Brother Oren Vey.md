# Brother Oren Vey

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Brother Oren is a monk and field healer of the [[03 Factions/Orders and Schools/Temple of the Still Palm|Temple of the Still Palm]]. The party found him held separately among the Cinder Ledger's captives and invited him aboard the Drowning Promise after his rescue.

He treated Vesper and Ryskulla with Lesser Restoration after disease exposure, cared for Korvash's severe abdominal wound, and trained Vesper during the voyage. He also examined Galdric Tombstone's condition and explained the dangers of the [[04 Lore/Discoveries/Bell of the Still Palm|Still Palm bell]].

After Abe's defeat, Oren remained in Tidemarch to attend to Vesper's affairs and repair the family home before making his way home. Xavier provided support for his needs and eventual passage. Completion of those tasks has not been reported.
