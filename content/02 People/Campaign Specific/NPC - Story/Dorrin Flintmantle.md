# Dorrin Flintmantle

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Dorrin Flintmantle was one of the four living captives rescued from the Ashen Tithe wagon in Forever Port. They were a dwarf stonemason with bruised ribs.

After his release in Forever Port, Dorrin worked as the blacksmith attached to the [[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Regions/Forever Port/Lion's Pride Inn|Lion's Pride Inn]]. He had earlier chosen to remain until [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Mara Fenlock|Mara Fenlock]] was safe at the Drow moon temple.
