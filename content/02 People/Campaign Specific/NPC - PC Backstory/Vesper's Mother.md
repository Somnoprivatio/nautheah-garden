# Vesper's Mother

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Vesper's mother once ran the general-goods shop whose former premises are now Tidemarch's [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch/Old Smokehouse|Old Smokehouse]].

When the crew found the laboratory manticore, Vesper said it might be their mother. The rebuilt creature was killed during the laboratory battle. The identification is preserved as Vesper's spoken suspicion.

Her personal name and spiritual fate have not been established in the shared account.
