# Wren Treadwater

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Wren is [[02 People/Campaign Specific/PC/Trifoot Everfold|Trifoot's]] sister, a female Tabaxi from Morning Dew. The party met her working under the name **Candice** at [[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Regions/Forever Port/Talontinous Delight|Talontinous Delight]]. She revealed white, black and gray striped fur, a long white braid, wave-patterned clothing and a blue pendant matching Trifoot's.

## What Wren told the crew

After working with blacksmiths, she located a ship that had taken a blacksmith's son and other Tabaxi, Vedalken and Kenku captives. She said she put alchemist's fire in its powder room and sank it, killing everyone aboard, then barely swam ashore. She described two or three weeks exposed to retaliation before [[02 People/World/NPC - Shop/Keelwhisper|Keelwhisper]] offered protection, lodging, pay, tools and arcane tutoring. These are Wren's accounts of events the crew did not witness.

## Work and knowledge

The crew saw her concealed workshop through the tavern cooler, opened by pulling a frozen lamb leg. Wren identified the four mechanical crows outside as her homunculi and described her work on the self-playing instruments and false crowd.

She supplied the crew's information about [[04 Lore/Discoveries/Selanora's Seeds|Selanora's Seeds]], [[04 Lore/Discoveries/Black Currents|Black Currents]] and the [[04 Lore/Discoveries/Moonlit Undertow|Moonlit Undertow]], and recommended a respectful introduction to the Circle of the Moon through Aeris Moonshadow. She later offered a floating disc, grape shot and chain shot; their delivery was not settled in that conversation.
