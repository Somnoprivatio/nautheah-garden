# Abe

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Abe was the experimenter encountered in a concealed laboratory near Tidemarch. He identified himself as Vesper's paternal grandfather and Father Jack's father. The party found Father Jack alive but altered in a vat and fought Abe through his first form and the Broken Seraph phase.

On **2 Harvestshade, Year 513**, Yama killed Abe. [[04 Lore/Deities/Active/Zaphkiel the Watcher|Zaphkiel the Watcher]] appeared, called Abe a fugitive of Mount Celestia and claimed his remains. The larger reasons for his fugitive status were not revealed to the crew.

The party subsequently left the laboratory. Xavier ignited the roots above it, and the great tree collapsed into the site once everyone was clear.
