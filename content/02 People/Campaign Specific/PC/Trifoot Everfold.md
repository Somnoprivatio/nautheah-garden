# Trifoot Everfold

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Trifoot is a male Tabaxi sailor from Morning Dew and captain of the [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Ships/Drowning Promise|Drowning Promise]]. The crew accepted his command after the mana-storm mutiny near the Vortex of Mist on Stormpeak 22, Year 513.

He travels with [[02 People/Campaign Specific/Companions/Luza|Luza]], wears a distinctive captain's tricorn and opposes slavery. [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - PC Backstory/Wren Treadwater|Wren Treadwater]] is his sister; the party met her in Forever Port. He paid Leroy's 300-platinum principal and completed the owed favor during [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 009 - The Queen Anne's Revenge Rises|Session 009]].

During the Underdark escape he held the cannons' fire to avoid announcing the ship's position. He later led the crew's farewell to Vesper with a flute performance at the burial at sea. In Lakeland, his orders allow shore leave with noon check-ins.
