# Yama

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Yama is the glaive-wielding ally who accompanied the crew during the confrontation with [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - PC Backstory/Abe|Abe]] near Tidemarch. His final strike killed Abe in the laboratory battle on 2 Harvestshade, Year 513.

He travels aboard the Drowning Promise and is included on its Lakeland arrival manifest. His formal place in the crew remains a conversation to have.

**Shared record:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 013 - Abe the Broken Seraph's Laboratory|Session 013]], [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 014 - Abe's Defeat and Vesper's Loss|Session 014]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 015 - Vesper's Farewell and Lakeland Arrival|Session 015]].
