# Vesper

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Vesper was an aasimar carnival strong-person and experienced shipwright from [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch|Tidemarch]]. They joined the Drowning Promise in Elpa, accepted the crew's piracy and opposition to slavery, and helped repair and maintain the ship. They and Jinx recognized one another from an earlier carnival performance.

Tidemarch brought reunions with [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Tobren Vale|Tobren Vale]] and [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Nix Copperthumb|Nix Copperthumb]]. Vesper's mother's former shop had become the Old Smokehouse. At the family home, the crew helped calm an uncontrolled transformation after Vesper found [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Father Jack|Father Jack]] in a distressed state.

Vesper died during the laboratory confrontation on **2 Harvestshade, Year 513**. Their body later returned to the ship and was preserved by a mercy-vessel cleric. The crew buried them at sea during the voyage to Lakeland. Falling rain stopped in midair and hands of seawater gently received the casket. Their unfinished note was found aboard ship.

[[04 Lore/Places Known by Report/Shadow Forge|Azul's earlier Shadow Forge report]] and the later return of the body are separate discoveries. The burial did not reveal a new fate for Vesper's soul.
