# Jinx

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Jinx is a masked carnival performer aboard the [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Ships/Drowning Promise|Drowning Promise]]. He performs with knives, fire, balance and misdirection. His manifest entry in Lakeland uses **Jasper**.

He acquired the [[04 Lore/Recovered Objects/Inverse Rapier of Verdict|Inverse Rapier of Verdict]] in Elpa, infiltrated the Cinder Ledger in disguise and confirmed living captives below deck. He later lit the fuse that destroyed the slaver vessel. During the Underdark escape, his magic helped free Vesper from an umber hulk.

At Tidemarch's [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch/Old Smokehouse|Old Smokehouse]], he gave a flaming-knife and circus-ball performance and agreed to a ten-percent venue arrangement with [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Veylan Giltbrush|Veylan Giltbrush]]. His anti-slavery advice later circulated widely enough to appear in Lakeland.
