# Xavier Fontaine

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]]

Xavier is one of the Drowning Promise's longest-serving crew members and an important officer under [[02 People/Campaign Specific/PC/Trifoot Everfold|Trifoot]]. The Lakeland questioning notices use his full name, **Xavier Fontaine**. A formal first-mate appointment has not been made.

He is a scholar and spellcaster, usually seen with spectacles, long dark hair and worn robes. He works with recovered books and spell scrolls. The group knows that Madruk Inkmaul's spellbook has a missing page; the search did not identify who removed it.

Xavier destroyed an umber hulk with Fireball during the Underdark escape and later burned the roots above Abe's laboratory so the tree collapsed after the party left. He helped fund Brother Oren's needs and eventual journey home. Jinx's widely circulated advice about avoiding slavery directs questions to Xavier.
