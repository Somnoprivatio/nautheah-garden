# Ocean Currents

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]] · [[01 World/World Overview|World Overview]]

The **Northern Current** and **Southern Current** are the two best-known long-distance ocean systems in Nautheah. Sailors use them to plan trade, migration, patrol routes, and crossings between otherwise distant coasts.

The currents are not merely lines on a chart: seasonal conditions, mana storms, island chains, and local waters can make entry or departure dangerous. The Vortex of Mist is an especially disruptive region.

Stories also speak of stranger moonlit or dark currents. Their routes, causes, and reliability are not general public knowledge and appear only in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Party-Known Discoveries|party-known discoveries]] where directly reported.
