# Ship and Property Bastion Capacity Guide

This house rule defines the maximum number of basic and special Bastion facilities that can be installed aboard ships or within land-based properties.

## Ship Tiers

### Tier 0 — Boat

- **Examples:** Skiff, Longboat, Launch
- **Basic Facilities:** 1–2
- **Special Facilities:** 0
- **Crew:** 1–4

### Tier 1 — Coastal Craft

- **Examples:** Cutter, Sloop, Schooner
- **Basic Facilities:** 3
- **Special Facilities:** 1
- **Crew:** 5–12

### Tier 2 — Working Vessel

- **Examples:** Brigantine, Caravel, Trireme, Small Airship
- **Basic Facilities:** 5
- **Special Facilities:** 2
- **Crew:** 10–25
- **Trireme Crew:** 30–50; rowers require increased crew

### Tier 3 — Galleon

- **Examples:** Galleon, Merchantman, Standard Airship
- **Basic Facilities:** 8
- **Special Facilities:** 2 maximum
- **Crew:** 25–60

### Tier 4 — Flagship

- **Examples:** Frigate, War Galleon, Large Airship
- **Basic Facilities:** 10
- **Special Facilities:** 4
- **Crew:** 50–90

### Tier 5 — Great Vessel

- **Examples:** Ship of the Line, Arcane Dreadnought, Sky Citadel
- **Basic Facilities:** 14
- **Special Facilities:** 5
- **Crew:** 90–160

### Tier 6 — Floating Fortress

- **Examples:** Floating Fortress, Leviathan Ship, Astral Barque
- **Basic Facilities:** 18
- **Special Facilities:** 6
- **Crew:** 150–300

## Land-Based Bastion Properties

### Small Housing Lot

- **Basic Facilities:** 3
- **Special Facilities:** 2
- **Typical Occupants:** 2–6
- **Limitations:**
  - Residential use only
  - One small workshop or private study
  - No Vast facilities
  - Cannot support a large public business or industrial operation

### Medium Housing Lot

- **Basic Facilities:** 6
- **Special Facilities:** 4
- **Typical Occupants:** 6–15
- **Limitations:**
  - Can support one small public-facing business
  - Can contain workshops, infirmaries, shrines, or similar facilities
  - Maximum of one Vast facility

### Mansion

- **Basic Facilities:** 10
- **Special Facilities:** 5
- **Typical Occupants:** 10–30
- **Limitations:**
  - Can contain 2 Vast facilities of each type (Basic and Special)
  - Can support laboratories, libraries, gardens, chapels, armories, and guest quarters
  - Not intended for large-scale warehousing or industrial production

### Commercial Lot

- **Basic Facilities:** 14
- **Special Facilities:** 6
- **Typical Occupants:** 20–60 staff and visitors
- **Limitations:**
  - Can support 2 Vast Special facilities and up to 4 Vast Basic facilities
  - Can include warehouses, shops, offices, workshops, and hireling quarters
  - Requires commercial permits, taxes, reputation, and/or local political relationships
