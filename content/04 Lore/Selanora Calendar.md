# Selanora Calendar

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

The Selanora Calendar is Nautheah's shared civil calendar. It contains **360 days**, divided into **ten months of 36 days**, with a repeating **five-day weekday cycle**.

> [!info] Current recorded date
> Calendaria is currently set to **Earthheart 8, Sunglade, Year 513**.

## Day and seasons

A day contains **24 hours**. Common dayparts are Morning beginning at 5:00, Noon at 10:00, Evening at 14:00, and Night at 18:00.

The natural year passes through **Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter**. Calendaria models these as equal recurring periods whose cycle does not align exactly with the 360-day civil year, allowing seasonal boundaries to move relative to named dates.

## Selanora's lunar cycle

[[04 Lore/Faiths of Nautheah#Selanora|Selanora]] follows a **36-day lunar cycle**, matching the length of one civil month. The named phases proceed in this order:

1. **Veilmoon** — the new-moon point.
2. **Pale Convergence** — the rising or waxing passage.
3. **Hunters Halo** — the full-moon point.
4. **Moonshadow Ascension** — the fading or waning passage.

Eclipses are considered rare. Lunar phases remain culturally important to sailors, Selanora traditions, and stories surrounding [[01 World/Great Regions and Islands#Selanora's Island|Selanora's Island]].

## Celestial frame

Calendar observers track **Nautheah**, its moon **Selanora**, the sun **Solanus**, and the other recorded planets **Ecpicto**, **Dwalintar**, and **Visikha**, a distant gas giant.
