# Cacophonous Chimera

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

The Cacophonous Chimera was one of the laboratory's hybrid creatures, combining a **giant rat, harpy and hyena**. Yama claimed it for Malar after Abe's defeat.

The claim is recorded, but its final disposition and specific harvested parts have not been settled. It is distinct from the rebuilt manticore chimera associated with Vesper's identification of their mother.
