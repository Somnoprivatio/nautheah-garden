# Shadow Forge

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

## What the party was told

After the laboratory battle, Azul told the assembled group that Vesper was dead, no longer on their plane and in the **Shadow Forge**. None of the group recognized the place or knew a route to reach it. This is the explanation they received, rather than a place they have visited.

Vesper's physical body later returned aboard the Drowning Promise and was buried at sea. The return establishes the body's later whereabouts; it did not reveal a new location or outcome for Vesper's soul.

**Shared record:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 014 - Abe's Defeat and Vesper's Loss|Session 014]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 015 - Vesper's Farewell and Lakeland Arrival|Session 015]].
