# Inactive Deities

[[04 Lore/Deities/Deities Index|Deities Index]] · [[04 Lore/Faiths of Nautheah|Faiths of Nautheah]]

These **147** names are retained as a player-visible religious reference catalogue. They are **not currently active Nautheah canon**. A listing does not establish a church, manifestation, historical role, regional following, or even confirmed existence within Nautheah.

A deity moves to the Active catalogue only after a reviewed local source, character connection, played event, or explicit GM ruling establishes the connection.
