# Malakar

[[04 Lore/Deities/Deities Index|Deities Index]] · [[04 Lore/Deities/Active/Active Deities Index|Active Deities Index]]

> [!info] Active public record
> Active means this deity already has a curated Nautheah connection. It does not automatically establish universal worship, direct intervention, or every claim from another setting.

Malakar is a Nautheah-specific shadow deity titled the Shadow Sorcerer of the Soul Forge of the Shadowfell. Esoteric traditions associate him with shadow, transformation, disputed fates of souls, and the danger of turning a soul into an object, weapon, servant, or performance.

No single public church, doctrine, or accepted account of his authority is currently established.
