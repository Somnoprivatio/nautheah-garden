# Zaphkiel the Watcher

[[04 Lore/Deities/Active/Active Deities Index|Active Deities]]

**Zaphkiel the Watcher** is a deity and the ruler and leader of Mount Celestia.

## Encounter with the Northern Current Carnies

After Yama killed Abe, a figure with a golden body and silver-and-gold wings appeared in a cone of brilliant light. He introduced himself as Zaphkiel the Watcher, called Abe a fugitive of Mount Celestia and thanked the group for defeating him. He claimed Abe's remains, which vanished in golden light.

He promised boons to Jinx, Trifoot, Xavier and Yama. Their effects and delivery remain unresolved.

The party's encounter revealed Abe's fugitive status. The charge and circumstances behind it were not explained.
