# Selune

[[04 Lore/Deities/Deities Index|Deities Index]] · [[04 Lore/Deities/Active/Active Deities Index|Active Deities Index]]

> [!info] Active public record
> Active means this deity already has a curated Nautheah connection. It does not automatically establish universal worship, direct intervention, or every claim from another setting.

Selune is the lesser moon goddess associated specifically with drow worship in Nautheah. She is distinct from Selanora, the goddess embodied by the world's moon. A drow moon temple in Forever Port and High Priestess Grace, Ilivara Myrynda, are publicly associated with this faith.

## Known temple and priestess

[[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Ilivara Myrynda|High Priestess Grace, Ilivara Myrynda]], serves at the [[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Regions/Forever Port/Drow Moon Temple|Drow Moon Temple in Forever Port]]. The temple cared for Mara Fenlock after the wagon rescue in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 005 - Captive Wagon and Cinder Ledger|Session 005]].
