# Selanora

[[04 Lore/Deities/Deities Index|Deities Index]] · [[04 Lore/Deities/Active/Active Deities Index|Active Deities Index]]

> [!info] Active public record
> Active means this deity already has a curated Nautheah connection. It does not automatically establish universal worship, direct intervention, or every claim from another setting.

Selanora is Nautheah's moon and a goddess of waves, the movement of the sea, guidance, transformation, natural cycles, and tides of magic. She appears as a silvery moon crossed by faint constellation-like streaks and shifting silver-blue patterns.

The public Triton account identifies Selanora as the daughter of Silvanus and Chauntea. Her murder stilled the world's waters until Chauntea preserved her soul in the moon and Silvanus joined the sealing rite. The restored tides then followed the moon. Seeds shed during the Moon Lock became the first Lunaveil trees.

Selanora is distinct from Selune, the lesser moon goddess associated specifically with drow worship in Nautheah.
