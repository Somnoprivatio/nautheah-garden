# Selûne

[[04 Lore/Deities/Deities Index|Deities Index]] · [[04 Lore/Deities/Active/Active Deities Index|Active Deities Index]]

> [!info] Active public record
> Active means this deity already has a curated Nautheah connection. It does not automatically establish universal worship, direct intervention, or every claim from another setting.

Selûne is retained as a separate imported or multiverse moon-power reference in the active catalogue. Nautheah's publicly established moon goddess is Selanora, while the unaccented Selune is the lesser moon goddess specific to drow worship. No broader public Nautheah doctrine for Selûne is currently established.
