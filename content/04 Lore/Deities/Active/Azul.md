# Azul

[[04 Lore/Deities/Deities Index|Deities Index]] · [[04 Lore/Deities/Active/Active Deities Index|Active Deities Index]]

> [!info] Active public record
> Active means this deity already has a curated Nautheah connection. It does not automatically establish universal worship, direct intervention, or every claim from another setting.

Azul is the goddess of rain and the seas. Farmers, sailors, and coastal communities honor her as a power of safe passage, necessary water, dangerous weather, and the ocean's changing temperament.

Her teachings emphasize respect for water, aid for people in genuine need, and opposition to pollution, poaching, and deliberate abuse of the sea. Her common symbol is a pale spiral resembling a whirlpool or typhoon. Morning Dew maintains a strong public tradition of Azul worship, and Northern Current Carnies accounts identify Trifoot Everfold as her first known champion.
