# Hanseath

[[04 Lore/Deities/Deities Index|Deities Index]] · [[04 Lore/Deities/Active/Active Deities Index|Active Deities Index]]

> [!info] Active public record
> Active means this deity already has a curated Nautheah connection. It does not automatically establish universal worship, direct intervention, or every claim from another setting.

Hanseath is honored as a deity of fellowship, brewing, courage, and honest celebration. The Swaying Peaks Brotherhood calls him the Laughing Brewer, the Keg-Bearer, and the Friend at the Long Table.

Brotherhood tradition treats a shared drink as a way to celebrate survival, mourn without shame, preserve truthful memories, and remember those who could not return. Its central observance is the First Pour: one cup for the labor that carried the gathering, one for the companions present, and one for the absent.
