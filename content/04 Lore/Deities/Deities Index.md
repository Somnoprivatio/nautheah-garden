## Classification boundary

**Active** means that a reviewed Nautheah source, public legend, character history, campaign record, or explicit setting ruling connects the deity to the world. It does not mean the deity is universally worshiped, currently alive, publicly understood, or known to intervene directly.

**Inactive** means the name is preserved for research and future worldbuilding, but no current Nautheah source activates it. Inactive entries should not be treated as confirmed churches, historical actors, or available patrons without later setting development.
