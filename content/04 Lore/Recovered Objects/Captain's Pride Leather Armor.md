# Captain's Pride Leather Armor

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

Captain's Pride is the seafoam green-blue leather armor displayed at The Second Skin and stolen by Trifoot while Jinx put Veyra Shellstitch to sleep.

Its visible design includes shifting color, gold trim, six gold abdominal buckles, three lines of gold chain, full-length sleeves, captain-like shoulders and an adjustable hood. The theft led to Leroy's payment-and-favor arrangement, settled in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 009 - The Queen Anne's Revenge Rises|Session 009]].

This entry records the object's appearance and history. Its wearer keeps the detailed attuned abilities on the character sheet.

**Acquired:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 002 - Elpa Heist, Enchantments, and a Curse|Session 002]].
