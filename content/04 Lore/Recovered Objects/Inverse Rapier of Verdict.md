# Inverse Rapier of Verdict

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

Jinx obtained this polished wooden rapier from Caldris Vane at Keelbreaker Blades. A crafting mishap inverted the original weapon's magic, so its condemn and commend effects both cause harm. A durability enchantment allows the wooden blade to function as a rapier.

**Acquired:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 002 - Elpa Heist, Enchantments, and a Curse|Session 002]].
