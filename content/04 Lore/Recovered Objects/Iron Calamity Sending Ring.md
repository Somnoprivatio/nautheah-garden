# Iron Calamity Sending Ring

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

Lieutenant Kaharvesh issued this small ring after the Iron Calamity questioned and cleared the Drowning Promise following the Cinder Ledger's destruction.

The crew identified its sole magical function as **Sending directly to the Iron Calamity**. It has no scrying or tracking function. Kaharvesh also told the crew to cite the vessel's prior questioning and clearance if challenged by another ship.

**Received:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 008 - Destruction of the Cinder Ledger|Session 008]]. **Identified:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 009 - The Queen Anne's Revenge Rises|Session 009]].
