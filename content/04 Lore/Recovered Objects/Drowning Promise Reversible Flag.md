# Drowning Promise Reversible Flag

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

Leroy left this red, blue and white reversible flag with a letter in the captain's quarters. Under the departure arrangement he described, its official orientation identifies the Drowning Promise as a merchant vessel to Elpa's authorities. Inverted, it displays the crew's pirate symbol.

**Received:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 002 - Elpa Heist, Enchantments, and a Curse|Session 002]].
