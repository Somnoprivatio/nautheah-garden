# Primordial Arcane Cannons

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

The Drowning Promise's forward-facing arcane cannons retain an active primordial magical charge and blue gemstones. Ryskulla observed primordial energy clinging to conductive metal during the opening storm; later examination established that the cannons remained charged after the storm's electrical charge had dissipated.

Their exact enchantment has not been identified. Ordinary shipboard cannon fire and these unusual forward cannons should not be assumed to have identical magical properties.

**Shared examinations:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 001 - The Storm Mutiny|Session 001]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 003 - A Charlatan's Bargain and Forever Port|Session 003]].
