# Rod of Encode Thoughts

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

A metal cylinder hidden in a newspaper sold to Jinx was identified as a single-use Rod of Encode Thoughts. The crew later examined its bulletin.

The work offered concerned goods, records, covert cargo transfers and merchant property. It explicitly excluded trafficking and trade in people. **No job request was submitted**, so the crew has no assigned job or pending representative from that exchange.

The sender and operator have not been identified in the shared account. The paperboy's courier role does not establish a connection to the wagon ambush or the Ashen Tithe.

**Shared record:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 004 - Wren Beneath the Keel and the Smuggler's Den Ambush|Session 004]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 005 - Captive Wagon and Cinder Ledger|Session 005]].
