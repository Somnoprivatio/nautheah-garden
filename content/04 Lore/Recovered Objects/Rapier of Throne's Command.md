# Rapier of Throne's Command

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

The command weapon taken from Black Caesar is a **Weapon of Throne's Command configured as a rapier**. It was earlier called the Rapier of Thrones. Trifoot completed attunement during the Tidemarch visit.

The weapon's identity does not make it a second item; the precise item rules are kept on the character sheet and shared rules reference.

**Recovered:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 007 - The Cinder Ledger Boarding|Session 007]]. **Current identification:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 012 - Tidemarch Homecoming|Session 012]].
