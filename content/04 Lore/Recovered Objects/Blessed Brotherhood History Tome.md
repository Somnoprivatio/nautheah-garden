# Blessed Brotherhood History Tome

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

Recovered from Galdric Tombstone's damaged pack, this glowing history tome froths like beer and carries a blessing. Holding it applies **Protection from Evil and Good** to the holder; releasing it ends that person's protection and passes the effect to the current holder.

Jinx touched the book and recoiled as though burned. The cause of his reaction was not established for the group.

The readable [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Galdric Tombstone Materials/Galdric Tombstone — Journal and History of the Swaying Peaks Brotherhood|Brotherhood history and journal]] preserve Galdric's travels, the Swaying Peaks traditions and the older stories available to the crew. Their devotional history and personal testimony should be read as those kinds of account.

**Recovered and examined:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 010 - Galdric and the Underdark Passage|Session 010]].
