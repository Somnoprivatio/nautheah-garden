# Tidecaller

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]] · [[04 Lore/Death of Selanora|Death of Selanora]]

Tidecaller is a legendary instrument associated with Selanora, the sea, and Triton memory. Stories credit it with music capable of reaching across water and answering the pull of the moon.

The instrument's complete powers, present location, and relationship to the Moon Lock are not part of the public record. References to Tidecaller should therefore be read as legend unless a campaign record identifies a witnessed effect.
