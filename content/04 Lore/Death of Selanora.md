# Death of Selanora

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]] · [[01 World/Great Regions and Islands#Selanora's Island|Selanora's Island]]

An old Triton account says Selanora died and that her death changed the relationship between moon, sea, and the peoples who depended upon them. The event is remembered through the **Moon Lock**, a great magical binding associated with the scattering of Selanora's Seeds and the transformation of lunar paths.

Public retellings connect the event to Selanora's Island, the moonlit routes that surround it, and living Lunaveil trees said to have grown from scattered seeds. The precise nature of Selanora, the mechanism of the Moon Lock, and the complete locations of the seeds remain matters of religion, legend, and active investigation.

The account is treated as established cultural history. Its supernatural interpretation may vary by community.
