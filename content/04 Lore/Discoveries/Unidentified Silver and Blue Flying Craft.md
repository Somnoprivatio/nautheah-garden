# Unidentified Silver and Blue Flying Craft

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

During the voyage to Lakeland, the crew saw an enormous silver-and-blue bird-shaped craft overhead. It appeared non-biological and carried blue circular devices. Trifoot plotted its course toward Lakeland.

The observation did not establish a vessel name, operator, destination landing or attack. It remains an unidentified flying craft in the crew's record.
