# Selanora's Seeds

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

The crew recovered a book and map of estimated seed locations from Maclear's quarters after the storm mutiny. The account connects the seeds to fragments of Selanora's power scattered after the Moon Lock.

## Wren's account

Wren said pirates have sought the seeds for generations and grow Selanora-linked trees aboard ships. According to her, the trees allow entry to the Black Currents and Moonlit Undertow for rapid travel, escape and smuggling. She warned that the seeds are difficult to grow and should be harvested without destroying the tree. She had seen a dead seed.

The crew's leads run through Aeris Moonshadow and the Circle of the Moon. Aeris offered conditional access and warned against abusing the seeds. Claims that seeds grant power or influence sea creatures remain beliefs and testimony, rather than a demonstrated set of effects.
