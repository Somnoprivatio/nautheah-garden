# Bell of the Still Palm

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

Brother Oren described a dangerous bell associated with his monastery. According to him, it tolls at intervals across different years and has previously continued for roughly a month without stopping.

He warned that prolonged exposure can cause psychic injury, madness or death within a range described as up to three days' travel. He had lost track of the cycle. The next tolling date, precise recurrence and a dependable refuge remain unknown.

This warning comes from Oren's account, rather than the Northern Current Carnies hearing the bell themselves.
