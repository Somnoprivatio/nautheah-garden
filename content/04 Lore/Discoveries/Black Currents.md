# Black Currents

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

Wren Treadwater told the crew that pirates use Selanora-linked trees aboard their ships to enter unusual routes called the **Black Currents**, allowing rapid travel, escape and smuggling.

Her account connects them with Selanora's Seeds and the Moonlit Undertow. It does not provide the crew with a tested chart, entrance procedure or reliable travel-time calculation. The party has not demonstrated these routes by using them.

**Source received:** Wren's testimony in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 004 - Wren Beneath the Keel and the Smuggler's Den Ambush|Session 004]]. See [[04 Lore/Ocean Currents|Ocean Currents]] for the ordinary Northern and Southern systems.
