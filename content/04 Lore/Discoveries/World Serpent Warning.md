# World Serpent Warning

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

An old lantern in the Underdark illuminated Undercommon words painted in black ichor:

> Be silent or you'll wake the world serpent.

Beyond the warning, the crew saw an immense purple worm suspended above the water in vast sheets of silk. Drider patrols crossed and guarded it. The worm was large enough to swallow the Drowning Promise, and the ship passed beneath it quietly without being detected.

The warning's name is not proof of a divine or cosmic identity. The crew knows the wording and the creature it saw. Earlier old worm traces and the worm described in Galdric's journal are separate observations; their identity has not been linked.
