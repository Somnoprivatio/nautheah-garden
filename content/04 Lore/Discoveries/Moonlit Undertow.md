# Moonlit Undertow

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

The **Moonlit Undertow** is the name Wren used for a Selanora-linked route associated with pirate travel and the trees grown from Selanora's Seeds. She described its usefulness for fast travel, smuggling and escape alongside the Black Currents.

This is testimony received by the crew, rather than an independently mapped passage. Its exact relationship to the Black Currents, entry conditions and risks remains unknown.

**Source received:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 004 - Wren Beneath the Keel and the Smuggler's Den Ambush|Session 004]].
