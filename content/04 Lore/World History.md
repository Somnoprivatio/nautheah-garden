# World History

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]] · [[05 Public Records/Public Records Index|Public Records]]

## Older remembered history

- The [[04 Lore/Death of Selanora|death of Selanora]] and the Moon Lock remain foundational to Triton and lunar-sea traditions.
- Selanora's Seeds are said to have scattered across Nautheah, with some becoming living Lunaveil trees.
- Continental powers, island nations, and maritime routes developed around lakes, currents, ports, and difficult magical regions.

## Recent public history

- Public commissions from the 2024 and 2025 job boards are now recorded as completed. Their high-level subjects are listed in [[05 Public Records/Completed Commissions|Completed Commissions]].
- Reports dated Earthheart 8, Year 513 describe disappearances and suspected forced labor in New Anglia. That threat remains unresolved.
- The Northern Current Carnies campaign began on Stormpeak 22, Year 513. Its party-shared history is kept in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Chronicle|Campaign Chronicle]].

This overview avoids assigning exact dates or causes where the surviving public notes do not provide them.
