# Faiths of Nautheah

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

Nautheah contains many faiths. This page lists only deities with a clear public connection to currently documented places or legends.

## Selanora

Selanora is a moon-and-sea figure central to Triton history, the [[04 Lore/Death of Selanora|Moon Lock]], Selanora's Island, the [[04 Lore/Tidecaller|Tidecaller]], and the calendar that bears her name.

## Azul

Azul is a sea deity honored especially by farmers and sailors of Morning Dew. Public tradition associates Azul with safe passage, restraint before the sea, and the responsibilities of people whose lives depend upon water.

## Regional faiths

- **Ilmater, Tyr, and Torm** have established public worship in Fali-City.
- **Oghma** is associated with Lakeland City's Church of Light and Knowledge and its learned institutions.

Other deities may be worshiped across the world, but a name's existence in a reference catalogue does not by itself establish an active public cult in every region.
