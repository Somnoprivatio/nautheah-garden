# Session 013 - Abe the Broken Seraph's Laboratory

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** After 6:57 AM on 2 Harvestshade, Year 513; exact endpoint unknown.

The crew reached Abe's concealed laboratory near Tidemarch with Yama. Abe identified himself as Vesper's paternal grandfather and Father Jack's father. Father Jack was found alive but altered in a vat.

The party fought Abe, overcame his first form and reached the Broken Seraph phase. The fight continued into the next session.
