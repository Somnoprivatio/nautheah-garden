# Session 009 - The Queen Anne's Revenge Rises

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** 22 Sunglade, Year 513; ending at 10:07 PM.

The crew investigated Madruk's missing spellbook page without finding it or identifying the culprit. The Iron Calamity ring was identified as Sending directly to that ship, with no tracking or scrying function.

In Elpa, Trifoot paid Leroy's 300-platinum principal. The owed favor was to take Black Caesar out to sea for Umberlee's claim. Leroy sent Israel Hands with the ship, and the crew was permitted shanties and fairer pay. Trifoot warned the others that Vorkharis knew of them and had issued a future challenge.

Israel taught sea shanties. Singing the Queen Anne's Revenge's song brought the blackened, waterlogged vessel up from the depths. Israel rejoined its crew. Using water breathing, the party followed below the sea as Caesar was chained to the mast and drowned. After his bones were cast into the water, the Revenge returned the party to the surface.

Israel warned them not to accept eternal sailing from Umberlee, then departed beneath the waves with his ship. **Leroy's principal and favor are both settled; Black Caesar is dead.**
