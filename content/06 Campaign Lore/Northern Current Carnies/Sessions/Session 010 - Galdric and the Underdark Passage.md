# Session 010 - Galdric and the Underdark Passage

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** 29 Sunglade, Year 513; ending at 2:00 AM.

The crew chose the thirteen-day Underdark passage over longer routes. They entered narrow, bioluminescent caverns and encountered unexplained voices, visions and a persistent song. During the broader voyage, they watched an enormous sperm whale defeat a ship-sized giant squid; Jinx won the betting pool.

Vesper rescued the dying dwarf Galdric Tombstone from shore. Brother Oren found disease, venom and poison that resisted available healing. At Galdric's request, the crew shared ale and revelry before his death.

His pack contained his journal, a blessed history tome, feast records and brewing notes. The tome's protection transfers to its current holder. Old purple-worm traces appeared along the route.

At a three-tunnel junction, four umber hulks landed aboard. The session ended before combat resolution.
