# Session 012 - Tidemarch Homecoming

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** 1-2 Harvestshade, Year 513; 8:00 AM to 6:57 AM the next morning.

Tobren Vale, Vesper's childhood friend and a halfling dockmaster, assigned the Drowning Promise a quieter berth at Nine Bells Quay. Hesta Pike of The Nine Bells described repeated disappearances of useful tradespeople while the authorities did little. Understaffed yards and unfinished work supported her warning. Nix left the ship to seek work at Gond's Nail.

Vesper's mother's former shop had become the Old Smokehouse, managed by Veylan Giltbrush. Jinx performed a flaming-knife and circus-ball act and negotiated the venue's ten-percent arrangement.

At the family home, Vesper found Father Jack drinking in a distressed state and suffered an uncontrolled transformation. The crew intervened; Xavier used Suggestion, and Vesper later healed Jack's forehead and carried him inside. Vesper took an addressed paper without reading it.

Brother Oren warned of the monastery bell's prolonged tolling and its dangerous reach. The crew rested aboard before an intended departure.
