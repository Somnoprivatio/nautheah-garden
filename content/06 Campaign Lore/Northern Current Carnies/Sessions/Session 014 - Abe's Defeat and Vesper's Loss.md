# Session 014 - Abe's Defeat and Vesper's Loss

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** 2 Harvestshade, Year 513; early morning, approximately 7:00 AM.

Yama killed Abe during the laboratory battle. Zaphkiel the Watcher appeared, called Abe a fugitive of Mount Celestia, thanked the group and removed his remains. The promised boons for Jinx, Trifoot, Xavier and Yama remain unresolved.

Trifoot killed Father Jack after Abe transformed him into a flesh golem. The rebuilt manticore chimera was also killed. The Cacophonous Chimera combined giant rat, harpy and hyena parts; Yama claimed it for Malar, with final disposition and harvest unsettled.

Vesper died. Azul told the assembled group that Vesper was no longer on their plane and was in the Shadow Forge. The party did not recognize the place or know how to reach it. How Vesper reached the place remained unknown to the group.

The later return and burial of Vesper's body are recorded in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 015 - Vesper's Farewell and Lakeland Arrival|Session 015]].
