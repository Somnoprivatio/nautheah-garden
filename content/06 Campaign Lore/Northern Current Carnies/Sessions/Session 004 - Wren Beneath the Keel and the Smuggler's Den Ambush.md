# Session 004 - Wren Beneath the Keel and the Smuggler's Den Ambush

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** Earthheart 8, Sunglade, Year 513; 8:40 AM to midday.

The waitress Candice revealed herself as Wren Treadwater, Trifoot's sister. She described the slaver ship she had destroyed and Keelwhisper's later protection. The crew saw her concealed workshop, mechanical crows, enchanted instruments and the tavern's false-crowd operation.

Wren gave testimony about Selanora's Seeds, pirate-grown trees, Black Currents and the Moonlit Undertow. She warned against entering the Circle's forest uninvited and recommended Aeris Moonshadow through the academy.

A newspaper concealed the single-use Rod of Encode Thoughts. In a back alley, the masked cart leader calling himself Eli and his group ambushed Jinx and Vesper. The rest of the party joined the fight. Vesper subdued the captain and captured the final fleeing bandit alive. Neither had been interrogated at the endpoint.
