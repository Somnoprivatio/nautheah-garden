# Session 005 - Captive Wagon and Cinder Ledger

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** 8 Sunglade, Year 513; midday to 7:10 PM.

The captain identified himself as Elias Morcant of New Anglia and the Ashen Tithe. His cell needed six captives; Jinx and Vesper were intended to join the four already hidden in the wagon. An oath-bound mercenary died while trying to name Vorkesh Sorn.

Eight rotating extra-dimensional chambers lay under the wagon's false floor. The party rescued Mara Fenlock, Dorrin Flintmantle, Sable Reedstep and Pell Underbough. The wagon carried the broken-chain-and-ember employer mark, a Cinder Ledger tide card, quota tallies, a red lantern and delivery cord. Mara received care at Ilivara Myrynda's Drow moon temple.

Keelwhisper explained the Cinder Ledger's Dock E46 arrival and departure window. The Gazette's report of Vorkharis taking living captives provided a wider investigative lead. The rod bulletin offered goods-only work; no application was sent. An academy message arranged a meeting with Aeris.
