# Session 002 - Elpa Heist, Enchantments, and a Curse

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** Stormpeak 22, Year 513; ending around 10 PM.

The crew visited Elpa's Keelbreaker Blades, Brined Crucible and Second Skin. They bought components, commissioned weapons, received an unusual stone intended for Ryskulla and arranged Jinx's fine clothes. Jinx obtained the Inverse Rapier of Verdict.

At The Second Skin, Jinx put Veyra Shellstitch to sleep and Trifoot stole Captain's Pride armor. The party escaped the guards. The shop was subsequently found ransacked; the later theft was not explained.

Leroy Jenk left a letter and reversible ship's flag, claiming service as Blackbeard's first mate. His payment-and-favor dispute with Trifoot continued into the next session.
