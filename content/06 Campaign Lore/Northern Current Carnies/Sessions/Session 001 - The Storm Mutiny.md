# Session 001 - The Storm Mutiny

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** Stormpeak 22, Year 513.

A violent mana storm struck near the Vortex of Mist while the ship returned from raids in Lakeland's outer waters. Trifoot cut Captain Maclear's tether and the captain fell into the sea. Maclear's loyal informant was later cut loose as well. The crew accepted Trifoot's command.

The crew recovered 400 platinum, magic gear, underwater travel scrolls, charts and a trapped chest from the captain's quarters. The book and map of Selanora's Seeds introduced estimated locations of seeds scattered after the Moon Lock. Ryskulla observed primordial mana around conductive metal aboard ship.

The Drowning Promise headed toward Elpa under its new captain.
