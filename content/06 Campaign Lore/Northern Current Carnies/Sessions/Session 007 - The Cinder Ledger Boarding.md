# Session 007 - The Cinder Ledger Boarding

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** 13 Sunglade, Year 513; ending at 3:10 PM.

The party used a false distress signal to draw in the Cinder Ledger. Trifoot, Ryskulla and Vesper swam beneath it and boarded through the captain's window. Madruk detected them but was overcome. Xavier boarded after the fight, when Black Caesar was being captured alive and manacled.

No Drowning Promise crew member was harmed in the boarding. Madruk and the remaining Cinder Ledger crew did not survive. The party found **100 living captives**, including the separately held Nix Copperthumb and Brother Oren Vey. The captives' origins were divided roughly among New Anglia, Whoasis, Lakeland and Fisherman's Detriment.

The party recovered Caesar's command rapier, weapons and tools, plus Madruk's spellbook, component pouch, activity ledger and maul. The damaged Cinder Ledger was lashed alongside the Drowning Promise while the party decided how to move the captives safely.

**New connections:** [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Nix Copperthumb|Nix Copperthumb]], [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Brother Oren Vey|Brother Oren Vey]] and [[04 Lore/Recovered Objects/Rapier of Throne's Command|the command rapier]].
