# Session 015 - Vesper's Farewell and Lakeland Arrival

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** 2-14 Harvestshade, Year 513; arrival-session endpoint 10:30 AM.

## Leaving Tidemarch

The crew gathered what they could carry from the laboratory. Xavier burned the roots overhead, and the great tree collapsed into the site after the party left. They inspected an empty hunting lodge but did not wait for its resident. Trifoot painfully recovered a cherished memory of stealing pies with Wren.

Rhask introduced himself as a self-appointed security officer and thanked them for handling the beast problem. The waiting group gave regional port and trade charts, fishing charts, a Whoasis smuggling-caravan route map and a fine net. Brother Oren stayed to attend to Vesper's affairs and repair the family home, supported by Xavier for his later passage.

Ryskulla's departure message and Vesper's unfinished note were found aboard. The crew investigated **18,000 missing gold**. Questioned sailors denied involvement under Zone of Truth, and Xavier volunteered; the thief remained unidentified.

## Voyage and farewell

Vesper's body returned aboard. A passing mercy-vessel cleric voluntarily cast Gentle Repose, and the crew later held a burial at sea. Korvash spoke the rites and Trifoot played a flute farewell. Rain stopped in midair and great hands of seawater gently received the casket.

An inexperienced helmsman scraped a coral embankment before Korvash corrected course. Hull repairs are now resolved. The crew saw an enormous silver-and-blue flying craft heading toward Lakeland. Fishing yielded **fifty rations** plus a separate large gulper eel with a numbing quality, retained for possible sale.

## Lakeland arrival

After a twelve-day voyage, the Drowning Promise arrived on **14 Harvestshade**. Three hours of verification preceded entry, then controlled currents guided it to an internal dock. The manifest included Yama and named Jinx as Jasper. Officials supplied local laws; Trifoot ordered shore leave with noon check-ins.

Five posters sought statements from Jinx, Trifoot, Vesper, Xavier Fontaine and Ryskulla about the Cinder Ledger and Morning Strait captives. They stated: **CRITICAL: THESE ARE NOT FUGITIVES DO NOT IMPRISON.** Jinx's anti-slavery advice had spread widely and still directed questions to Xavier.

A paperboy advertised the Red Light Carnival's **A lovely little terrible joke** for tonight, pointing to red lights and a large red tent beyond the city road. Removed notices reappeared on the enchanted board. The historical session endpoint was **10:30 AM**, after the arrival sequence.
