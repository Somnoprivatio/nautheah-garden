# Session 006 - Infiltrating the Cinder Ledger

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** 9 Sunglade, Year 513; ending at 1:32 PM.

The crew damaged the Cinder Ledger's rudder to delay it. Jinx infiltrated in a bugbear disguise, saw living captives packed below deck and escaped after discovery. This session did not free them.

The party recovered a route map covering Whoasis, New Anglia, Lakeland, the Simic Isles, Prominence and Fisherman's Detriment, along with official port-protection letters and evidence of a locked black ledger. The route's order and ledger contents were not established.

At the academy meeting, Aeris verified the crew's account and offered conditional Circle access, warning against hostility and seed misuse. Malithar Windrider and Nana Mori became known academy contacts. Academy magic returned the party to the Drowning Promise.

**Connections:** [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Aeris Moonshadow|Aeris]], [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Malithar Windrider|Malithar]], [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Nana Mori|Nana]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Recovered Evidence|recovered evidence]].
