# Session 008 - Destruction of the Cinder Ledger

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** 19 Sunglade, Year 513; ending at 5:51 AM.

The crew supplied food, water and treatment to the captives, moving survivors to a beach treeline camp. Brother Oren treated disease exposure with Lesser Restoration and accepted a place aboard as a healer. Nix accepted work as tidewright and hull-patch crafter.

After recovering useful supplies and records, the party towed the Cinder Ledger away from the strait. Jinx ignited its fuse and powder. **The slaver vessel was completely destroyed.**

Black Caesar's reported testimony described earlier Blackbeard service and a split over slavery. The group speculated about Leroy's identity, without confirming it.

The Iron Calamity investigated the noise. Lieutenant Kaharvesh accepted the crew's cover story and provided a passage/clearance ring. Captain Maelin Veyr was named as the vessel's captain.
