# Session 003 - A Charlatan's Bargain and Forever Port

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** Stormpeak 22 through Earthheart 8, Sunglade, Year 513; ending at 8:40 AM.

The armor dispute became a 300-platinum principal and one future favor. Vesper joined the Drowning Promise after learning the truth about its piracy and opposition to slavery. Three other sailors were hired, bringing the documented complement to 39 NPC crew and five adventurers.

The crew provisioned for ten days. Storehouse and Barracks construction began, Vesper performed shipwright work, and examination confirmed that the forward arcane cannons retained their primordial charge. Trifoot and Ryskulla collected their enchanted weapons. Veyra completed Jinx's outfit from personal stock after the ransacking prevented further work.

The Drowning Promise sailed three days to Forever Port. At Talontinous Delight, Keelwhisper accepted Maclear's delayed cargo, arranged unloading and revealed an old acquaintance with Korvash. The group discussed Selanora's Seeds and possible Circle of the Moon contacts.
