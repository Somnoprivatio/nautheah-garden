# Session 011 - Beneath the World Serpent

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] | [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Session Index|Session Index]]

**In-world date:** 29 Sunglade through 1 Harvestshade, Year 513; ending at 8:00 AM.

The crew escaped the umber-hulk ambush. Additional hulks in the side tunnels carried away two unnamed sailors, whose fate remains unknown. Korvash suffered a severe abdominal wound and was taken to Brother Oren. Jinx's Sleep freed Vesper from a hulk, and Xavier destroyed the floating creature with Fireball. Trifoot held the loaded broadside to avoid announcing their position.

An old lantern revealed the Undercommon warning, **"Be silent or you'll wake the world serpent."** The ship then passed quietly beneath an immense purple worm suspended in silk and guarded by driders.

After emerging, the crew traveled seven days toward New Anglia. Korvash recovered, visible ship damage was repaired and fishing supplied provisions. Brother Oren trained Vesper. Vesper and Xavier discussed eventually returning Galdric's history to its monastery. Caesar's encoded book still appeared to discuss foreign cheeses; Xavier recognized a deliberate cipher.

The Drowning Promise reached Tidemarch at **8:00 AM on 1 Harvestshade**.
