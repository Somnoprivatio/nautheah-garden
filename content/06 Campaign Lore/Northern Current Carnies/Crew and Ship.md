# Crew and Ship

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

## Drowning Promise

The Drowning Promise is the party's vessel. Trifoot took command during the Stormpeak mana storm. By the end of Session 003, the ship carried **39 non-player crew and five player characters**, for 44 people aboard.

The crew purchased ten days of provisions before leaving Elpa. Construction began on a Storehouse and Barracks, each estimated to require five days. The ship carries forward-facing arcane cannons with primordial magical charge and blue gemstones; their exact enchantment is unknown.

## Active player characters

- [[02 People/Northern Current Carnies#Trifoot Everfold|Trifoot Everfold]] — captain.
- [[02 People/Northern Current Carnies#Xavier|Xavier]] — senior officer; no formal first-mate appointment is recorded.
- [[02 People/Northern Current Carnies#Jinx|Jinx]]
- [[02 People/Northern Current Carnies#Ryskulla|Ryskulla]]
- [[02 People/Northern Current Carnies#Vesper|Vesper]] — shipwright and strong-person.

Captain Maclear, the former captain, died during the mutiny. The exact contents of his delayed cargo remain unknown even though its delivery at Forever Port was accepted.
