# Crew and Ship

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

## Drowning Promise

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Ships/Drowning Promise|Drowning Promise]] is the party's vessel, captained by Trifoot since the storm mutiny. It is currently docked inside Lakeland City. Hull repairs are resolved, while the 18,000-gold theft remains unexplained. The forward primordial arcane cannons still lack a full identification.

The old 39-NPC-plus-five-adventurer complement describes the end of Session 003. Later departures, losses and arrivals mean it is not a current census.

## Active adventurers and traveling ally

- [[02 People/Campaign Specific/PC/Trifoot Everfold|Trifoot Everfold]]: captain and Tabaxi sailor.
- [[02 People/Campaign Specific/PC/Xavier Fontaine|Xavier Fontaine]]: scholar, spellcaster and senior officer.
- [[02 People/Campaign Specific/PC/Jinx|Jinx]]: carnival performer; Jasper on the Lakeland manifest.
- [[02 People/Campaign Specific/PC/Yama|Yama]]: glaive-wielding ally from the laboratory confrontation; formal crew place pending.

## Crew and companions

- [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Korvash Brinewake|Korvash Brinewake]]: experienced Simic deckhand, recovered from the Underdark wound.
- [[02 People/Campaign Specific/Companions/Luza|Luza]]: Trifoot's pseudodragon companion.
- Two unnamed sailors were carried into Underdark tunnels and remain missing; death is unconfirmed.

## Former and absent companions

- [[02 People/Campaign Specific/PC/Vesper|Vesper]]: shipwright and strong-person, deceased; buried at sea.
- [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Ryskulla|Ryskulla]]: left to search for her family and intends to send word when she has a lead.
- [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Nix Copperthumb|Nix Copperthumb]]: rescued tidewright who left to seek work in Tidemarch.
- [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Brother Oren Vey|Brother Oren Vey]]: rescued healer who stayed in Tidemarch to attend to Vesper's affairs.
- [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Captain Maclear|Captain Maclear]]: former captain, killed in the storm mutiny.

## Other vessels

### Cinder Ledger

The [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Ships/Cinder Ledger|Cinder Ledger]] is destroyed. Its one hundred living captives were liberated from the ship and moved to a beach treeline camp. Its larger trafficking network remains unresolved.

### Iron Calamity

The [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Ships/Iron Calamity|Iron Calamity]] is an Iron Authority naval vessel commanded by Maelin Veyr. Kaharvesh conducted the crew's questioning at sea and supplied a Sending ring.

### Queen Anne's Revenge

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Ships/Queen Anne's Revenge|Queen Anne's Revenge]] rose in response to its shanty, carried Caesar to his underwater execution and returned the party safely before descending again.
