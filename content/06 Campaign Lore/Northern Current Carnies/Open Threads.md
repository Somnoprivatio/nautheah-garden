# Northern Current Carnies Open Threads

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

## Locally active questions

- **The trafficking network:** What do the wagon evidence, protection papers and Sorn connection reveal beyond the destroyed Cinder Ledger? Do Tidemarch's disappearances connect to that operation?
- **The 18,000-gold theft:** Who took the ship's money? The questioning has not identified the thief.
- **Yama's crew place:** What role will the ally from the laboratory take aboard the Drowning Promise?
- **Red Light Carnival:** What will the crew find if they attend the show advertised outside Lakeland tonight?

## Continuing arcs

- **Selanora's Seeds:** Follow the book, map and Wren's testimony through Aeris's conditional Circle access.
- **Ryskulla's family:** Await her useful lead and decide how to help when she sends word.
- **The missing sailors:** Two people were carried into Underdark tunnels; their fate remains unknown.
- **Vesper and the Shadow Forge:** The body was recovered and buried, but the group's report leaves the soul's fate and the unfamiliar place unresolved.
- **The Second Skin:** Identify the later ransacking's culprit and motive.
- **Madruk's missing page:** Determine what is missing and who removed it.
- **Vorkharis's warning:** Trifoot told the crew the Ash Sovereign knows of them and offered a future challenge.
- **Galdric's legacy:** Preserve the recovered history and consider returning it to the Swaying Peaks Brotherhood.
- **Zaphkiel's promise:** The promised boons' effects and delivery remain unresolved.
- **The Still Palm bell:** Establish its cycle and a safe approach before a monastery visit.

## Settled outcomes

The Cinder Ledger encounter, Leroy's principal and favor, Black Caesar's fate and the laboratory's destruction have recorded outcomes. Repairs, routine purchases and material gathering are kept with ship and item records.
