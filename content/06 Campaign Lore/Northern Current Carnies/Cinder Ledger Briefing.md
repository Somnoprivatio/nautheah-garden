# Cinder Ledger Briefing

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] · [[02 People/Black Caesar|Black Caesar]] · [[02 People/Madruk Inkmaul|Madruk Inkmaul]] · [[02 People/Vorkesh Sorn|Vorkesh Sorn]] · [[02 People/Vorkharis|Vorkharis]] · [[01 World/Forever Port|Forever Port]]

The **Cinder Ledger** is an unresolved vessel lead in the Northern Current Carnies investigation. Current party records associate the ship with **Black Caesar** as captain and **Madruk Inkmaul** as first mate and arcane scribe.

[[02 People/Vorkesh Sorn|Vorkesh Sorn]] is the publicly identified head of Forever Port's [[03 Factions/Iron Authority|Iron Authority]] naval military. His official role is established; the public record does not establish personal involvement in the Cinder Ledger's alleged crimes.

## What the party can responsibly say

- The ship's name is connected to the party's investigation into disappearances, forced labor, and coastal transfers reported by the Forever Port Gazette.
- The vessel is relevant to the wider Vorkharis/Ash Sovereign inquiry, but the public record does not prove that every report was ordered by Vorkharis personally.
- The exact cargo, route order, receiving site, crew complement, and current position remain unresolved in the public-facing chronicle.
- The party damaged the vessel's rudder and confirmed living captives aboard, but no completed interception, rescue, trial, or sinking is recorded here.

## Command information

The public record does not confirm the Cinder Ledger's full command chain. Future testimony or recovered records may establish how the ship fits into the wider Vorkharis investigation.

## Related mission paths

- Compare future ship evidence with the [[05 Public Records/Issues/Earthheart 8, Year 513 - Vorkharis Takes the Living|Gazette report]].
- Protect and corroborate witnesses rather than treating a single allegation as a complete chain of command.
- Determine whether the Cinder Ledger is a transport, a collection vessel, or one link in a larger route.
- Keep the Selanora seed inquiry separate from the Vorkharis investigation unless the party obtains evidence connecting them.
