# Cinder Ledger Briefing

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

The Cinder Ledger investigation established a real capture-and-transport operation. The vessel is **destroyed**, its one hundred captives were moved off the ship, and Black Caesar is dead. The wider system remains an open investigation.

## Outcome

The crew fed and treated survivors, moved them to a beach treeline camp and destroyed the stripped vessel in open water. Nix and Oren accepted places aboard the Drowning Promise. The group's known follow-up does not supply every survivor's final destination.

Caesar was later taken to sea for Umberlee's claim and executed aboard Queen Anne's Revenge. Leroy's principal and favor were settled by that journey.
