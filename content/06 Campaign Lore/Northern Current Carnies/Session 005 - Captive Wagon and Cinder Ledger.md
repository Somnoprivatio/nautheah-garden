# Session 005 — Captive Wagon and Cinder Ledger

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] · [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Chronicle|Campaign Chronicle]] · [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Open Threads|Open Threads]]

## What happened

- The party questioned a surviving ambush captain and searched the wagon used in the Forever Port attack.
- A hidden compartment contained **four living captives**, confirming that the attackers were taking people rather than ordinary cargo.
- The evidence connected the operation to the wider [[02 People/Vorkharis|Vorkharis]] forced-labor investigation and identified the **Cinder Ledger** as a related vessel lead.
- The rescued captives were moved toward safety and care. Their personal histories remain private to their own records and are not reproduced here.
- The party reviewed the public report [[05 Public Records/Issues/Earthheart 8, Year 513 - Vorkharis Takes the Living|Vorkharis Takes the Living]].
- Trifoot analyzed a **Rod of Encode Thoughts** found in the newspaper. It described goods-only work; **no job request was submitted**.
- An academy message opened a route toward [[02 People/Aeris Moonshadow|Aeris Moonshadow]] and Circle of the Moon knowledge.

## End-of-session state

The party had evidence of a forced-labor network and a likely Cinder Ledger connection, but had not yet intercepted the vessel. The investigation remained active at the end of the session.
