# Northern Current Carnies Campaign Chronicle

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

## Session 001 — The Storm Mutiny

**Stormpeak 22, Year 513.** A violent mana storm struck near the Vortex of Mist. Trifoot removed Captain Maclear, who fell into the sea and died, and the crew accepted Trifoot as captain. The party recovered funds, unusual gear, charts, and records concerning Selanora's Seeds.

## Session 002 — Elpa Heist, Enchantments, and a Curse

The party visited Elpa's public shops and commissioned equipment. Trifoot stole Captain's Pride armor from Veyra Shellstitch after Jinx put her to sleep. Elpa authorities pursued the group; Leroy Jenk later demanded payment and a favor. The Second Skin was subsequently found ransacked by another party. The session ended around 10 PM.

## Session 003 — A Charlatan's Bargain and Forever Port

**Earthheart 8, Year 513, 8:40 AM.** Trifoot and Leroy settled the armor dispute at 300 platinum plus one future favor. Vesper joined. The crew provisioned, left Elpa, completed a three-day voyage, and arrived at Forever Port. Keelwhisper accepted Maclear's delayed cargo at Talontinous Delight.

## Session 004 — Wren Beneath the Keel and the Ambush

Wren Treadwater, Trifoot's sister, revealed herself to the party while using the name Candice. She described her protected work for Keelwhisper and shared Selanora-linked travel knowledge. Afterward, Jinx received a newspaper containing an unused Rod of Encode Thoughts. A masked group ambushed Jinx and Vesper in an alley. The other three joined the fight; the captain and one bandit were captured alive.

The chronicle stops at **midday on Earthheart 8**. No later preparation is presented as an accomplished event.
