## Sessions 001-003: a new captain and the Elpa dispute

The storm mutiny removed Maclear and put Trifoot in command. The crew found Selanora's Seeds records, reached Elpa and commissioned equipment. The theft of Captain's Pride armor led to Leroy's 300-platinum-and-favor arrangement; the shop's later ransacking remained unexplained. Vesper joined the ship, the commissioned weapons and Jinx's clothes were collected, and Keelwhisper accepted Maclear's delayed cargo at Forever Port.

**Records:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 001 - The Storm Mutiny|Session 001]], [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 002 - Elpa Heist, Enchantments, and a Curse|Session 002]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 003 - A Charlatan's Bargain and Forever Port|Session 003]].

## Sessions 004-006: Wren and the captive route

Wren revealed herself at Talontinous Delight, shared her slaver-ship account and described seeds, trees and unusual currents. The alley ambush led to Elias Morcant, the Ashen Tithe and four living captives hidden beneath a wagon. The oath-bound death while naming Sorn, the employer mark and Cinder Ledger handoff became linked clues. The crew confirmed captives below deck, recovered route and protection papers, and reached Aeris and the academy.

**Records:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 004 - Wren Beneath the Keel and the Smuggler's Den Ambush|Session 004]], [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 005 - Captive Wagon and Cinder Ledger|Session 005]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 006 - Infiltrating the Cinder Ledger|Session 006]].

## Sessions 007-009: the slaver ship falls

The crew boarded the Cinder Ledger, captured Caesar and found one hundred living captives. They supplied care, moved survivors to shore and destroyed the vessel. Nix and Oren accepted work aboard. Kaharvesh's Iron Calamity contact provided a ring later identified as Sending only. Leroy's principal was paid, and his favor ended with Caesar's execution aboard the risen Queen Anne's Revenge. Israel rejoined the legendary vessel after warning the party about Umberlee's offer of eternal sailing.

**Records:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 007 - The Cinder Ledger Boarding|Session 007]], [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 008 - Destruction of the Cinder Ledger|Session 008]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 009 - The Queen Anne's Revenge Rises|Session 009]].

## Sessions 010-012: the deep passage and Tidemarch

Galdric Tombstone died in the shared revelry he requested, leaving his journal and blessed Brotherhood history. The crew survived umber hulks but lost two sailors into the tunnels, then passed quietly beneath a gigantic webbed worm guarded by driders. At Tidemarch, Hesta described disappearances, Nix sought work, Jinx performed at the Old Smokehouse and the crew helped calm Vesper at the family home. Oren warned them about the Still Palm bell.

**Records:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 010 - Galdric and the Underdark Passage|Session 010]], [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 011 - Beneath the World Serpent|Session 011]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 012 - Tidemarch Homecoming|Session 012]].

## Sessions 013-014: Abe and Vesper's loss

The laboratory discovery revealed Abe as Vesper's paternal grandfather and placed Father Jack alive in a vat. The fight continued until Yama killed Abe. Father Jack died after transformation into a flesh golem. Zaphkiel claimed Abe's remains and promised unresolved boons. Vesper died, and Azul told the group that Vesper was in the unfamiliar Shadow Forge.

**Records:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 013 - Abe the Broken Seraph's Laboratory|Session 013]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 014 - Abe's Defeat and Vesper's Loss|Session 014]].

## Session 015: farewell and Lakeland arrival

The crew left the laboratory before the tree collapsed into it, received Tidemarch's thanks and found Ryskulla's message. Oren stayed behind. The 18,000-gold theft remained unexplained. Vesper's recovered body was preserved and buried at sea, where hands of water received the casket. After twelve days, the ship entered Lakeland on 14 Harvestshade. The crew found questioning notices explicitly rejecting fugitive status, Jinx's circulating advice and the Red Light Carnival's advertisement for that evening.
