# Session 006 — Infiltrating the Cinder Ledger

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] · [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Chronicle|Campaign Chronicle]] · [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Cinder Ledger Briefing|Cinder Ledger Briefing]]

## What happened

- The crew damaged the Cinder Ledger's rudder, delaying the vessel without sinking it.
- Jinx infiltrated the ship and confirmed that living captives were being held below deck. No captives were freed during this session.
- Trifoot recovered a route map, official port-protection papers, and evidence of a locked black ledger. The route's exact order and the ledger's contents remain unknown.
- Azul reaffirmed that she hates slavers and wants the Cinder Ledger destroyed, but she cannot destroy the vessel herself. This is Azul's current warning and assessment.
- The crew returned to the Drowning Promise, one day from Forever Port, with the Cinder Ledger still an active lead.

## End-of-session state

No interception, rescue, sinking, or locked-ledger examination was completed. The party must decide how to pursue the vessel while preserving evidence and protecting the captives.
