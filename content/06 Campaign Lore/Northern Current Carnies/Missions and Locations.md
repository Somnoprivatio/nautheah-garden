# Campaign Missions and Locations

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]] · [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Chronicle|Campaign Chronicle]] · [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Open Threads|Open Threads]]

This page collects the active party's public-facing mission leads and locations reached in the Northern Current Carnies campaign. It separates completed travel from unresolved objectives.

## Locations reached

- **Vortex of Mist / Stormpeak:** The opening mana-storm mutiny occurred near this dangerous magical region.
- **Elpa, City of the Two Tides:** The party provisioned, commissioned equipment, resolved the Leroy Jenk obligation, and departed aboard the Drowning Promise.
- **Forever Port:** The party arrived after a three-day voyage and visited [[01 World/Forever Port#Talontinous Delight|Talontinous Delight]].
- **Talontinous Delight:** The party completed Keelwhisper's contact exchange, arranged Maclear's delayed cargo delivery, and received leads concerning Selanora's Seeds.

New Anglia is a reported destination in the Vorkharis investigation, not a completed location visit in this public chronicle.

## Active mission paths

### Leroy Jenk's obligation

The party owes Leroy Jenk a 300-platinum principal and one future favor. Full payment within the first month avoids interest; partial payment accrues interest on the principal balance.

### Drowning Promise refit

The crew is completing the Storehouse and Barracks aboard the Drowning Promise and continues to investigate the ship's unusual forward arcane cannons.

### Selanora's Seeds

The party is seeking reliable information about Selanora's Seeds, the Black Currents, and the Moonlit Undertow. Wren identified Aeris Moonshadow and the Fali-City Adventuring Academy as possible routes toward Circle of the Moon knowledge. Malithar Windrider is an official academy mentor and a public contact for that route.

### Vorkharis and the Cinder Ledger

The Forever Port Gazette's New Anglia report created a public investigative lead involving disappearances, forced labor, coastal transfers, and volcanic work sites. The Cinder Ledger is a related unresolved vessel lead. The party has not publicly established its complete route, cargo, command structure, or final destination.

### Forever Port evidence

The party's open lines of inquiry include the newspaper and Rod of Encode Thoughts received after leaving Talontinous Delight, the ambush evidence, and any future testimony that can corroborate the Gazette's allegations. Unused or unsubmitted job-board messages remain separate from the Vorkharis investigation.

## Public boundary

This page records travel, mission leads, and public or party-shared knowledge. It does not publish private character memories, hidden item identities, unplayed rescue outcomes, or unapproved commander biographies.
