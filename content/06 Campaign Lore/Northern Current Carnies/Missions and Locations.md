## Known destinations and reports

The Circle of the Moon remains a conditional lead through Aeris. The Temple of the Still Palm and Swaying Peaks floating temple are known destinations; this crew has not reached them. The Shadow Forge is known from Azul's report, without a discovered route.

## Resolved obligations

Leroy's principal and favor are settled. The Cinder Ledger is destroyed, Black Caesar is dead and the laboratory was destroyed after the crew escaped. Hull repairs are resolved. These outcomes do not close the wider trafficking network, missing workers or remaining mysteries.
