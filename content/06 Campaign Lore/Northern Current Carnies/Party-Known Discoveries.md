# Party-Known Discoveries

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

## Selanora's Seeds

Maclear's recovered book and map gave estimated seed locations. Wren connected pirate-grown trees to unusual routes, and Aeris offered conditional Circle access. The crew has leads and testimony, rather than tested growing and travel rules. See [[04 Lore/Discoveries/Selanora's Seeds|Selanora's Seeds]].

## Unusual currents

[[04 Lore/Discoveries/Black Currents|Black Currents]] and the [[04 Lore/Discoveries/Moonlit Undertow|Moonlit Undertow]] are names and route claims supplied by Wren. The ordinary Northern and Southern Current systems remain separate geographic knowledge.

## Primordial magic aboard the Drowning Promise

The [[04 Lore/Recovered Objects/Primordial Arcane Cannons|forward arcane cannons]] retain primordial charge and blue gemstones. Their complete enchantment remains unidentified.

## Forever Port clues

The wagon ambush exposed an [[03 Factions/Networks/Ashen Tithe|Ashen Tithe]] capture cell and four living captives. The group's quota, eight hidden chambers, employer mark and tide card connected the wagon to the Cinder Ledger. An oath-bound mercenary died while attempting to name Vorkesh Sorn. The [[04 Lore/Recovered Objects/Rod of Encode Thoughts|rod bulletin]] offered goods-only work and received no application.

## Cinder Ledger and Vorkharis lead

The party confirmed slavery aboard the vessel, boarded it, found one hundred living captives and later destroyed it completely. Caesar was captured and subsequently executed; Madruk did not survive the boarding. The survivors were moved to shore, while the wider network and final safe destination remain open in shared follow-up.

The Gazette's allegations, wagon employer mark, Sorn revelation, route map and port-protection papers form connected evidence. They do not supply a complete command chart. See [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Cinder Ledger Briefing|Cinder Ledger Briefing]].

## Queen Anne's Revenge

The party saw the ship rise when its shanty was sung, followed it beneath the ocean and witnessed Caesar's death. Israel rejoined its crew and warned against eternal sailing with Umberlee. The group's witnessed encounter is recorded on [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Ships/Queen Anne's Revenge|the vessel page]].

## Galdric Tombstone and the Swaying Peaks

Galdric's final revelry, the blessed tome and the recovered writings link the crew to the [[03 Factions/Orders and Schools/Swaying Peaks Brotherhood|Swaying Peaks Brotherhood]]. His journal describes Nocte Equi Trojani, Nimble, drow liberators, purple worms and the clearing of Mount Neveah's mists. The older stories are received history and testimony, not new eyewitness experiences of this crew.

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Galdric Tombstone Materials/Galdric Tombstone — Journal and History of the Swaying Peaks Brotherhood|Read the journal and Brotherhood history]]; the companion feast and brewing volumes remain available below.

## Underdark and missing sailors

The crew escaped umber hulks, but two sailors were carried into tunnels and were not recovered. Korvash survived after Oren's care. The [[04 Lore/Discoveries/World Serpent Warning|World Serpent warning]] preceded the silent passage beneath a gigantic worm guarded by driders.

## Tidemarch and the Still Palm

Hesta reported waves of disappearances, and the crew saw understaffed yards and unfinished work. Vesper's former family shop had become the Old Smokehouse. Oren's [[04 Lore/Discoveries/Bell of the Still Palm|bell warning]] added a dangerous uncertainty to the monastery destination.

## Abe, Zaphkiel and Vesper

The laboratory revealed Abe's family connection and Father Jack alive in a vat. Yama killed Abe; Zaphkiel removed his remains and promised unresolved boons. Father Jack died after transformation into a flesh golem, and Vesper died during the confrontation. Azul told the group that Vesper was in the unfamiliar [[04 Lore/Places Known by Report/Shadow Forge|Shadow Forge]]. The body later returned and was buried at sea; no new soul outcome was revealed.

## Lakeland arrival

The ship entered Lakeland on 14 Harvestshade after a twelve-day voyage. The questioning notices explicitly reject fugitive status. Jinx's anti-slavery advice has circulated, and the [[03 Factions/Performing Companies/Red Light Carnival|Red Light Carnival]] advertises its show for that evening. The silver-and-blue flying craft remains unidentified.

## Missing gold

The missing amount is **18,000 gold**. Questioned sailors denied involvement under Zone of Truth, but the inquiry did not establish the thief. Hull repairs are already resolved and are not an active mystery.
