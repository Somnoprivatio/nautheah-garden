# Party-Known Discoveries

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

## Selanora's Seeds

Records recovered from Captain Maclear's quarters describe estimated locations of legendary seeds scattered after the Moon Lock. Wren later provided additional testimony about Selanora-linked routes. The locations and full effects remain unresolved.

## Unusual currents

The party received testimony concerning dark or moonlit currents whose behavior differs from the well-known Northern and Southern Currents. These reports are party knowledge, not a complete public chart.

## Primordial magic aboard the Drowning Promise

The crew's investigation established that the forward arcane cannons retain a primordial magical charge and blue gemstones. Their exact enchantment has not been identified.

## Forever Port clues

The crew analyzed a single-use Rod of Encode Thoughts found in a newspaper. It described goods-only work and excluded people trafficking; no job request was submitted. The wagon used in the ambush was searched, and four living captives were recovered and moved toward safety.

## Cinder Ledger and Vorkharis lead

The party's investigation identified the **Cinder Ledger** as a slaving vessel associated with the Gazette's reports of disappearances, forced labor, coastal transfers, and volcanic work sites tied to the title **Ash Sovereign**. The Northern Current Carnies intercepted and ultimately destroyed the vessel, rescued the people held aboard, and disrupted this part of the Iron Authority's operation. The broader networks serving Vorkesh and Vorkharis remain unresolved.

See [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Cinder Ledger Briefing|Cinder Ledger Briefing]].

## Galdric Tombstone and the Swaying Peaks

During the Drowning Promise's Underdark passage, the crew rescued **Galdric Tombstone**, a gravely injured dwarf monk and librarian of the [[01 World/Prominent Regions/Mount Neveah/Swaying Peaks Brotherhood/Floating Home Temple|Swaying Peaks Brotherhood]]. His wounds, disease, venom, and poison resisted the healing available aboard ship. At his request, the crew gave him ale and shared a final revelry before his death.

Galdric's damaged pack contained a glowing, beer-frothing Brotherhood history tome, his journal, feast records, and detailed brewing notes. The surviving materials have been reconstructed for the crew:

- [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Galdric Tombstone Materials/Galdric Tombstone — Journal and History of the Swaying Peaks Brotherhood|Journal and History of the Swaying Peaks Brotherhood]]
- [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Galdric Tombstone Materials/Galdric Tombstone — Reveler's Feast|The Reveler's Feast]]
- [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Galdric Tombstone Materials/Galdric Tombstone — Underdark Insect and Venom Brewing Notes|Underdark Insect and Venom Brewing Notes]]

The recovered history links Galdric to an earlier Underdark journey with **Nocte Equi Trojani**, a battle against a purple worm and her brood, the gnome Nimble and a traveling drow liberation band, and the clearing of the mists remembered as the **Rebirth of Mount Neveah**.
