# Party-Known Discoveries

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

## Selanora's Seeds

Records recovered from Captain Maclear's quarters describe estimated locations of legendary seeds scattered after the Moon Lock. Wren later provided additional testimony about Selanora-linked routes. The locations and full effects remain unresolved.

## Unusual currents

The party received testimony concerning dark or moonlit currents whose behavior differs from the well-known Northern and Southern Currents. These reports are party knowledge, not a complete public chart.

## Primordial magic aboard the Drowning Promise

The crew's investigation established that the forward arcane cannons retain a primordial magical charge and blue gemstones. Their exact enchantment has not been identified.

## Forever Port clues

Jinx possesses a single-use Rod of Encode Thoughts hidden in a newspaper. At the campaign cutoff it remains unused, and its sender and encoded content are unknown. The ambushers' cart remains unsearched.
