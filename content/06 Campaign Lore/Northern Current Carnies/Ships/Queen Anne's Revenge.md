# Queen Anne's Revenge

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

Queen Anne's Revenge is the legendary vessel associated with Blackbeard. Leroy claimed past service as its first mate, and Black Caesar reported sailing with Blackbeard's pirates.

## What the party witnessed

When Israel Hands led the Drowning Promise's crew in the ship's sea shanty, the Revenge rose from the ocean. Its hull looked blackened, burned and waterlogged, while its sailors appeared healthy despite the sea. Israel recognized the ship and returned aboard.

The party followed under water breathing to witness Black Caesar, chained to the mast, drown beneath the ocean. The crew cast his cleaned bones into the water. Thousands of sea-creature eyes watched from the dark; Israel said they could not approach to feed.

The ship brought the party back to the surface, and Israel warned them against accepting eternal sailing from Umberlee. The Revenge then sank beneath the sea again.

The crew knows this manifestation and its connection to Umberlee. The ship's complete origin, the terms of its service and the reason for its condition are not established as shared knowledge.
