# Iron Calamity

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

The Iron Calamity is a major [[03 Factions/Authorities/Iron Authority|Iron Authority]] naval vessel commanded by Captain Maelin Veyr, with Lieutenant Kaharvesh as a known officer. Its whitewashed wooden hull, iron bands, white sails and towering battery decks distinguish it from the much smaller Drowning Promise.

Its towering silhouette and broadside batteries make it visibly larger than the Drowning Promise.

Kaharvesh questioned the Drowning Promise after the Cinder Ledger explosion, accepted its cover story and supplied a ring used for direct Sending to the Iron Calamity. The encounter made the vessel a known contact in the ongoing investigation, without settling the private knowledge or loyalties of its officers.

**Shared encounter:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 008 - Destruction of the Cinder Ledger|Session 008]]; ring identification in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 009 - The Queen Anne's Revenge Rises|Session 009]].
