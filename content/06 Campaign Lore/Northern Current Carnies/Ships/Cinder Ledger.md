# Cinder Ledger

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

The Cinder Ledger was a slaving vessel described as between a sloop and a galleon in size. It was commanded by Black Caesar with Madruk Inkmaul as first mate and arcane scribe. The Forever Port wagon investigation led the party to its scheduled handoff at Dock E46.

The crew first damaged its rudder and confirmed living captives below deck. Later, they lured it toward a false distress call and boarded through the captain's window. Caesar was captured alive; Madruk and the remaining ship's crew did not survive. The party discovered **100 living captives**, including Nix Copperthumb and Brother Oren Vey.

After food, water and treatment, the survivors were moved to a beach treeline camp. The party stripped useful supplies and records, towed the ship away from the strait and ignited its remaining powder. **The Cinder Ledger is completely destroyed.**

The wider network and survivors' final safe destination have not been fully established in shared follow-up. See [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Cinder Ledger Briefing|the linked evidence and outcome briefing]].
