# Drowning Promise

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

The Drowning Promise is the crew's vessel, formerly commanded by Maclear and now captained by Trifoot Everfold. The Northern Current Carnies are reshaping its raiding history into a traveling maritime carnival while opposing slavery.

## Crew and fittings

The documented complement after Vesper joined was **39 NPC crew plus five adventurers, 44 people total**. That is a historical count. Later events included the loss of two sailors into Underdark tunnels, Nix's departure, Ryskulla's departure, Vesper's death, Oren staying in Tidemarch and Yama traveling aboard. A new complete headcount has not been established.

The ship carries [[04 Lore/Recovered Objects/Primordial Arcane Cannons|forward arcane cannons]] with blue gemstones and an unidentified primordial enchantment, a map room and Leroy's reversible merchant-and-pirate flag. Storehouse and Barracks work began in Elpa; the first construction schedule is a historical record, not a current plot objective.

## Current shared state

The ship arrived at Lakeland City on **14 Harvestshade, Year 513**, after twelve days at sea. Local authorities verified the manifest and guided it to an internal dock. Hull repairs are resolved. Trifoot ordered shore leave with noon check-ins.

The **18,000-gold theft** remains unexplained. Questioned sailors denied involvement under Zone of Truth, and Xavier volunteered; the questioning did not identify the thief. The current fishing catch produced fifty rations plus a separate gulper eel with a numbing quality, retained for possible sale.

Vesper's physical body was preserved and buried at sea during this voyage. The crew's known associates and former members are listed in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Crew and Ship|Crew and Ship]].
