# Iron Authority

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]] · [[01 World/Forever Port|Forever Port]] · [[02 People/Vorkesh Sorn|Vorkesh Sorn]]

The **Iron Authority** is Forever Port's naval military. It patrols the harbor and surrounding waters, supports maritime defense, and enforces the port's authority at sea. Its head is [[02 People/Vorkesh Sorn|Vorkesh Sorn]].

## Public role

- Maintains naval patrols around Forever Port.
- Responds to maritime threats and harbor emergencies.
- Supports enforcement, inspections, and security for a city that operates around the clock.

The Authority's wider command structure, deployments, and relationship to the current Ash Sovereign investigation remain matters of public inquiry.
