# Nocte Equi Trojani

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

Nocte Equi Trojani is the adventuring company described in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Galdric Tombstone Materials/Galdric Tombstone — Journal and History of the Swaying Peaks Brotherhood|Galdric Tombstone's recovered journal]]. Galdric traveled with them from Lakeland City through the Underdark.

His account includes a purple worm and her brood, a meeting with Nimble and drow liberators, and witnessing the clearing of Mount Neveah's mists. The journal does not claim that the company caused the clearing.

This is history received through Galdric's writings, rather than a present-day meeting or alliance with the Northern Current Carnies.
