# Traveling Drow Liberators

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Galdric Tombstone Materials/Galdric Tombstone — Journal and History of the Swaying Peaks Brotherhood|Galdric's journal]] describes a traveling band of drow fugitives, guides and liberators encountered with the gnome Nimble during Nocte Equi Trojani's Underdark journey.

According to the account, they supplied food, opened escape routes and guided families away from Lolth's control. **Traveling Drow Liberators** is a descriptive label for the people in that account, rather than an established formal organization name. Their present whereabouts are unknown.
