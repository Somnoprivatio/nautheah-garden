# Fali-City Adventuring Academy

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]] · [[01 World/Fali-City and New Anglia|Fali-City]]

The Fali-City Adventuring Academy is a recognized school connected to several adventurers and older campaigns. Its public purpose is the education and preparation of people who undertake dangerous travel, exploration, and commissioned work.

Publicly named contacts include [[02 People/Malithar Windrider|Malithar Windrider]], a wizard and mentor, and [[02 People/Nana Mori|Nana Mori]], a receptionist. The curriculum, leadership, admissions rules, and wider present roster have not yet been confirmed for public release. Individual character histories tied to the Academy remain in their campaign records unless made public in play.
