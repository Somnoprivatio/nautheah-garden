# Temple of the Still Palm

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]] · [[01 World/Temple of the Still Palm|Temple location]] · [[04 Lore/Faiths of Nautheah#Eldath|Eldath]]

The Temple of the Still Palm is an old New Anglian monastic order devoted to mastery of body, breath, emotion, and deliberate action. Temple tradition places its founding nearly nine centuries ago.

The order honors **Eldath** through teachings of peace, still water, and conscious restraint. Its monks do not teach that anger must be denied. Instead, anger must become still enough to be understood and directed rather than obeyed.

Training includes meditation, breath discipline, repetitive work, controlled martial practice, and learning to stop once force is no longer necessary. Senior members may also travel beyond the monastery for periods of service and contemplation.

The order's present leadership, complete membership, internal ranks, and private rites are not part of the public record.
