# Fisherman's Detriment Port Authority

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

The Fisherman's Detriment Port Authority is the regional name for the [[03 Factions/Authorities/Iron Authority|Iron Authority]]. The two names refer to the same organization.

Its visible responsibilities include harbor security, vessel inspection, naval patrols and port-guard functions. Leroy Jenk serves in Elpa's main port guard; the Iron Calamity is a known naval vessel. See the main Iron Authority entry for the crew's discoveries and known officers.
