# Iron Authority

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

The Iron Authority is Forever Port's naval military, also recorded regionally as the **Fisherman's Detriment Port Authority**. Its public head is [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Vorkesh Sorn|Vorkesh Sorn]]. It patrols harbors and surrounding waters, conducts inspections, enforces maritime authority and responds to threats at sea.

## Known structure

- [[03 Factions/Authorities/Iron Legion|Iron Legion]]: a specialized naval-soldier group within the Authority.
- [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Ships/Iron Calamity|Iron Calamity]]: a naval vessel commanded by Captain Maelin Veyr, with Lieutenant Kaharvesh serving aboard.
- Leroy Jenk: a captain in Elpa's main port guard.

## Discovered by the crew

The Ashen Tithe interrogation linked Sorn's name to a binding oath and a captive handoff. The party recovered official port-protection letters from the Cinder Ledger. Those clues support the [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Cinder Ledger Briefing|ongoing investigation]]; the complete internal chain and each officer's involvement remain unresolved.

After the Cinder Ledger's destruction, Kaharvesh questioned the Drowning Promise, accepted its cover story and supplied a passage/clearance ring. Identification later established the ring as a direct Sending device to the Iron Calamity, with no tracking or scrying function.

Lakeland's later notices request statements about the Cinder Ledger and the Morning Strait captives. They explicitly say the crew are not fugitives and must not be imprisoned.
