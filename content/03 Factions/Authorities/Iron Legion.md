# Iron Legion

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

The Iron Legion is a specialized group of naval soldiers within the [[03 Factions/Authorities/Iron Authority|Iron Authority]]. The parent organization, its soldiers and the ship **Iron Calamity** are distinct references.

The Legion's name appears in connection with Lakeland's request for a statement about the Cinder Ledger and the Iron Calamity. The questioning notices are not arrest warrants.
