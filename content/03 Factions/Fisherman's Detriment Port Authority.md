# Fisherman's Detriment Port Authority (Iron Authority)

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

The **Fisherman's Detriment Port Authority** is the regional name for the [[03 Factions/Iron Authority|Iron Authority]], Forever Port's naval military. These are one organization, not separate authorities. Its visible responsibilities include harbor security, courthouse and port-guard functions in Elpa, vessel inspection, and armed patrols.

The Authority operates heavily armed vessels capable of controlling port approaches. The party's distant Session 003 sighting was the **Iron Calamity**, commanded by [[02 People/Captain Maelin Veyr|Captain Maelin Veyr]] with [[02 People/Lieutenant Kaharvesh|Lieutenant Kaharvesh]] as a known officer. [[02 People/Civic and World Figures#Leroy Jenk|Leroy Jenk]] serves as a captain in Elpa's main port guard. Deeper jurisdiction and internal structure remain open.
