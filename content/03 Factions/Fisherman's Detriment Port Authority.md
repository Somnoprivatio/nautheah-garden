# Fisherman's Detriment Port Authority

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

The Fisherman's Detriment Port Authority is a public maritime authority operating across the island nation's ports. Its visible responsibilities include harbor security, courthouse and port-guard functions in Elpa, vessel inspection, and armed patrols.

The Authority operates heavily armed vessels capable of controlling port approaches. Leroy Jenk serves as a captain in Elpa's main port guard. The exact national chain of command, jurisdiction beyond the ports, and relationship between local guards and fleet officers have not yet been published.
