# Circle of the Moon

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

The Circle of the Moon is a recognized druidic tradition with ties to lunar observation, wilderness knowledge, and the Moonshadow family. Elder Sylvanos and Sylvaris Moonshadow are publicly associated with that tradition.

Older notes propose a five-member council, but the membership, offices, powers, and political reach of that council are not yet locked as public canon. This page therefore records only the Circle's confirmed existence and known associations.
