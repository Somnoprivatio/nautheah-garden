# Ashen Tithe

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

The Ashen Tithe is the mercenary organization identified during the Forever Port wagon interrogation. Its work includes taking living people as "merchandise." [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Elias Morcant|Elias Morcant]] led the cell that tried to abduct Jinx and Vesper.

## Exposed operation

- The cell's quota was six living captives. Four were hidden in the wagon; the two isolated party members were intended to complete it.
- The wagon contained eight rotating extra-dimensional chambers beneath a false floor. Four chambers held living people.
- A tide card pointed to the Cinder Ledger at Dock E46. The party also found quota marks, a hooded red lantern and a red cord used for deliveries.
- The broken-chain-and-ember mark identified Vorkharis as the employer for this job, according to Keelwhisper.
- An oath-bound mercenary died while trying to name Vorkesh Sorn.

Keelwhisper described the Tithe as a mercenary organization operating through New Anglia and Whoasis. The exposed cell and vessel establish a trafficking operation; the complete membership, receiving sites and wider command chain remain investigative questions.
