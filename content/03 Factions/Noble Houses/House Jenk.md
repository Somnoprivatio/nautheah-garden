# House Jenk

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

[[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Regions/Elpa/Elpa|Elpa]]'s House Jenk is publicly represented in the crew's records by [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Leroy Jenk|Leroy Jenk]], formally styled **Leroy of House Jenk**, a captain in the main port guard.

The house's wider roster, holdings and alliances have not been established. Leroy's armor-theft agreement with Trifoot was settled through the paid principal and completed favor in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 009 - The Queen Anne's Revenge Rises|Session 009]].
