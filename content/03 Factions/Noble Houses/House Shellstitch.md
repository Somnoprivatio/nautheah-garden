# House Shellstitch

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

House Shellstitch is an important family in Elpa's hierarchy. [[02 People/World/NPC - Shop/Veyra Shellstitch|Veyra Shellstitch]], proprietor of The Second Skin, is a prominent member. Her precise parentage and rank have not been established.

The family's residence is in Elpa's lower underwater city. The later ransacking of Veyra's shop remains unexplained, separate from the party's earlier theft of Captain's Pride armor.
