# Fali-City Adventuring Academy

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

The Fali-City Adventuring Academy is a recognized school preparing adventurers for dangerous travel, exploration and commissioned work. It operates a contact outpost in Forever Port.

## Known contacts

- [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Malithar Windrider|Malithar Windrider]]: wizard and mentor to Aeris Moonshadow and his adventuring party.
- [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Nana Mori|Nana Mori]]: receptionist handling academy messages and appointments.
- [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Aeris Moonshadow|Aeris Moonshadow]]: Circle of the Moon druid contacted through the academy.

The Northern Current Carnies used its message network to arrange the Aeris meeting. Afterward, academy magic teleported them back to the Drowning Promise.
