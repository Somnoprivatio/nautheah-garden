# Circle of the Moon

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

The Circle of the Moon is a recognized druidic tradition associated with lunar observation, wilderness knowledge and the Moonshadow family. Elder Sylvanos and Sylvaris Moonshadow are known connections; Aeris Moonshadow is Sylvaris's son and Sylvanos's grandson.

## Information given to the crew

Wren warned that entering the Circle's forest without invitation could leave intruders lost and in mortal danger. She recommended a respectful introduction through Aeris.

At the academy meeting, Aeris verified the crew's account and granted conditional access, warning against hostility or abusing Selanora's Seeds. The crew has a route toward the Circle, rather than a completed forest expedition or a demonstrated set of seed-growing rules.
