# Swaying Peaks Brotherhood

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

The Swaying Peaks Brotherhood is a mountain order of Drunken Fist monks, brewers, archivists, couriers, cooks and keepers of stories. It honors [[04 Lore/Deities/Active/Hanseath|Hanseath]], called the Laughing Brewer, Keg-Bearer and Friend at the Long Table.

Its [[01 World/Prominent Regions/Mount Neveah/Swaying Peaks Brotherhood/Floating Home Temple|floating home temple]] travels among Mount Neveah's shifting peaks. The recovered history emphasizes balance, fellowship, truthful memory and bringing fellow travelers safely home. Its four remembered duties are the Open Hand, Moving Foot, Kept Cask and Spoken Name.

The First Pour offers a cup for the work that made the gathering possible, shares another among companions and remembers the absent with the final cup. Historical ales, recipes and communal readings help preserve the order's memories.

## Connection to the Northern Current Carnies

The crew rescued the dying librarian [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Galdric Tombstone|Galdric Tombstone]] and shared his requested final revelry. His journal, blessed history tome, feast instructions and brewing notes are held aboard the ship. Vesper and Xavier discussed eventually returning that history to the Brotherhood.

[[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Galdric Tombstone Materials/Galdric Tombstone — Journal and History of the Swaying Peaks Brotherhood|The recovered journal and history]] are the source for these traditions and for Galdric's earlier expedition with Nocte Equi Trojani.
