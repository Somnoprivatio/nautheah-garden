# Temple of the Still Palm

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

The Temple of the Still Palm is an old monastic order in New Anglia's smaller mountain country. Tradition places its founding nearly nine centuries ago. It honors [[04 Lore/Deities/Active/Eldath|Eldath]] through mastery of body, breath, emotion and deliberate action.

Its teaching holds that anger should become still enough to understand and direct. Training combines meditation, breath discipline, repetitive work, controlled martial practice and the ability to stop when force is no longer necessary. Senior members may undertake journeys of service and contemplation.

The public seal is an upright open hand holding a round drop of water, surrounded by five unbroken rings.

## Brother Oren's account

[[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Brother Oren Vey|Brother Oren Vey]], rescued from the Cinder Ledger, is a monk and field healer of the order. He described a [[04 Lore/Discoveries/Bell of the Still Palm|dangerous bell]] that can toll for prolonged periods and harm people within several days' travel. He had lost track of its cycle, so the crew lacks a dependable next tolling date.

The [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Smaller Mountain Country/Temple of the Still Palm|mountain temple]] remains a known destination, rather than a completed visit by the crew. Oren stayed in Tidemarch after Vesper's death before his eventual journey home.
