# Red Light Carnival

[[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

The Red Light Carnival is the traveling entertainment advertised outside Lakeland City. Its current notice promotes **A lovely little terrible joke** for **tonight**, the evening of the crew's arrival on **14 Harvestshade, Year 513**.

A newspaper boy directed visitors toward the red lights and large red tent beyond the city road. The local enchanted board replaces removed handbills. The crew has seen the advertisements but has not attended or performed at the carnival.
