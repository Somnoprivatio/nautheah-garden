# Underdark Water Passage

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

The Drowning Promise chose the Underdark as a dangerous thirteen-day alternative to longer sea routes. Narrow waterways, bioluminescent caverns, uncertain footing and the danger of drow attacks shaped the passage.

## Encounters and warnings

- Unexplained voices, visions and a persistent song affected the crew. Their source was not identified.
- Galdric Tombstone was found dying on an underground shore and brought aboard.
- Old purple-worm traces appeared before the later encounter with a living giant worm.
- Umber hulks boarded the ship. Two unnamed sailors were carried into side tunnels and were not recovered; their deaths remain unconfirmed.
- The crew passed silently beneath an enormous purple worm suspended in webs and guarded by drider patrols after seeing the warning [[04 Lore/Discoveries/World Serpent Warning|"Be silent or you'll wake the world serpent."]]

After emerging, the crew traveled seven days toward New Anglia, reaching Tidemarch at 8:00 AM on 1 Harvestshade, Year 513.
