# Morning Strait

[[01 World/Places Index|Places Index]] · [[01 World/Fisherman's Detriment|Fisherman's Detriment]] · [[01 World/Fisherman's Detriment#Morning Dew|Morning Dew]]

The **Morning Strait** is the named water passage between the island settlement of [[01 World/Fisherman's Detriment#Morning Dew|Morning Dew]] and the mainland of [[01 World/Fisherman's Detriment|Fisherman's Detriment]]. It is a recognized coastal route for fishing boats, ferries, and vessels moving between the island village and the main continent.

Two working approaches are associated with the strait: **Dewbreak Anchorage** on the island side and **Straitwatch Quay** on the mainland side. Their local officials, hazards, and harbor customs remain open for future exploration.

## Dewbreak Anchorage

Dewbreak Anchorage is the island-side approach to Morning Strait, used by local fishing boats, ferries, and visiting vessels.

## Straitwatch Quay

Straitwatch Quay is the mainland-side approach to Morning Strait, serving as a practical landing and departure point for the crossing.
