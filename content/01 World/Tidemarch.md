# Tidemarch

[[01 World/Places Index|Places Index]] · [[01 World/Fali-City and New Anglia#New Anglia|New Anglia]] · [[01 World/Crosswater Reach|Crosswater Reach]]

Tidemarch is a working shipbuilding town on the westernmost settled coast of New Anglia. Its deep tidal inlet opens onto the Crosswater Reach, making the town one of the last dependable New Anglian harbors used by westbound vessels.

Sailors call the area the **border coast**. The expression refers to the edge of reliable New Anglian settlement and maritime authority, not a literal land border with Whoasis.

## Harbor and Appearance

Salt marshes, low wind-cut cliffs, and farmland surround the harbor. The inlet mud is nearly black, while old pilings and sea walls are stained with tar, smoke, and salt. Most buildings use dark timber over fieldstone foundations, with steep pitch-treated roofs built to withstand coastal storms.

The town begins work before sunrise. Caulking hammers, ropewalk crews, gulls, harbor bells, timber wagons, and fishing boats make the waterfront noisy long before the markets open.

## Trade and Government

Tidemarch builds and repairs ships and handles grain, lumber, tools, livestock, sailcloth, rope, and imported goods. A harbor reeve formally represents New Anglian authority, but captains, shipwrights, warehouse owners, and old harbor families carry substantial local influence.

## Landmarks

- **The Low Quays:** tidal docks used by fishing boats, coastal traders, and vessels needing urgent repair.
- **The Long Yard:** the principal shipbuilding district, with multiple slips, tool sheds, and ropewalk access.
- **Westbreak:** a stone-and-timber breakwater extending into the Reach.
- **The Saltmarket:** a canvas-covered market for provisions, maritime supplies, and foreign cargo.
- **Lantern Hill:** the higher district overlooking the harbor.
