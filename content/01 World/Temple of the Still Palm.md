# Temple of the Still Palm

[[01 World/Places Index|Places Index]] · [[01 World/Fali-City and New Anglia#New Anglia|New Anglia]] · [[01 World/Brackenford|Brackenford]] · [[03 Factions/Temple of the Still Palm|Monastic order]]

The Temple of the Still Palm occupies a high cliff among New Anglia's smaller mountain spines and cold, misted valleys. An old foothill and pilgrimage road rises toward it from the Brackenford region, although exact travel times vary with weather and the condition of the mountain paths.

## Landscape

Generations of monks have deliberately shaped the surrounding slopes. Stone training terraces cross the mountainsides. Prayer arches span narrow paths, prayer flags stretch across chasms, and bells hang from cliff faces where changing winds can set them sounding.

In clear weather, the temple commands long views across the lower mountains. Mist gathers in the valleys below but does not permanently obscure the monastery.

## Architecture

The monastery combines tiered stone pagodas, open-air meditation courtyards, symmetrical gardens, bridges connecting isolated training towers, and large hanging bells. Water channels slow into still basins before leaving the grounds, reinforcing the temple's emphasis on composure and balance.

The site's age, mountain setting, disciplined reputation, and association with Eldath are publicly known. Its internal trials, private vows, and personal histories are not included in this public account.
