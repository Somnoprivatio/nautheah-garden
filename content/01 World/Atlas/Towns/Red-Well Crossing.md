# Red-Well Crossing

[[01 World/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Sunscar Expanse|Sunscar Expanse]]

**Red-Well Crossing** is a publicly recognized Town within [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Sunscar Expanse|Sunscar Expanse]].
It is known for waystation hospitality and guarded wellhouses.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **5800**.
Magic access is described publicly as **very limited; practical low-level magic is guarded around cisterns**.
The local resource profile is **water and finished goods are rationed and convoy-controlled**.

Specific officials, private faction structures, and adventure hooks are kept out of this public summary.
