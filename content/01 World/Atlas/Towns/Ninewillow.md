# Ninewillow

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Reedwater Basin|Reedwater Basin]]

**Ninewillow** is a publicly recognized Town within [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Reedwater Basin|Reedwater Basin]].
It is known for quiet healing springs and riverside pilgrimage.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **6800**.
Magic access is described publicly as **abundant around canals and cities; scholarly and industrial magic is widely traded**.
The local resource profile is **freshwater agriculture, barges, metalwork, and research goods**.

Specific officials, private faction structures, and adventure hooks are kept out of this public summary.
