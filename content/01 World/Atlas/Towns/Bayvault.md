# Bayvault

[[01 World/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Southglass Bay|Southglass Bay]]

**Bayvault** is a publicly recognized Town within [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Southglass Bay|Southglass Bay]].
It is known for bonded warehouses and honest ledgers.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **18000**.
Magic access is described publicly as **abundant; arcane services, reagents, and enchanted goods are common**.
The local resource profile is **well supplied by roads, workshops, and layered trade**.
