# Brightbarrow

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Starfen Plateau|Starfen Plateau]]

**Brightbarrow** is a publicly recognized Town within [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Starfen Plateau|Starfen Plateau]].
It is known for hill forts, sunstones, and seasonal fairs.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **5100**.
Magic access is described publicly as **abundant around canals and cities; scholarly and industrial magic is widely traded**.
The local resource profile is **freshwater agriculture, barges, metalwork, and research goods**.
