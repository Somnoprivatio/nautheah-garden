# Lockstep

[[01 World/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Westmere Trade March|Westmere Trade March]]

**Lockstep** is a publicly recognized Town within [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Westmere Trade March|Westmere Trade March]].
It is known for canal engineering and tollhouse politics.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **19000**.
Magic access is described publicly as **abundant around canals and cities; scholarly and industrial magic is widely traded**.
The local resource profile is **freshwater agriculture, barges, metalwork, and research goods**.

Specific officials, private faction structures, and adventure hooks are kept out of this public summary.
