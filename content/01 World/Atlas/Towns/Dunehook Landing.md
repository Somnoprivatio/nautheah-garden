# Dunehook Landing

[[01 World/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Glasswind Coast|Glasswind Coast]]

**Dunehook Landing** is a publicly recognized Town within [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Glasswind Coast|Glasswind Coast]].
It is known for cliff anchorage and salvage.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **4200**.
Magic access is described publicly as **moderate near ports; new anglia trade brings occasional enchanted goods**.
The local resource profile is **sea-glass, coastal imports, and salt; inland routes remain constrained**.
