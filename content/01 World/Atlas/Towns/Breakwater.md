# Breakwater

[[01 World/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Glassmarch Coast|Glassmarch Coast]]

**Breakwater** is a publicly recognized Town within [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Glassmarch Coast|Glassmarch Coast]].
It is known for naval repair and uneasy ceasefires.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **18000**.
Magic access is described publicly as **unstable and scarce; magic is feared, weaponized, or warped by occult forces**.
The local resource profile is **war-rationed supplies, salvage, and contaminated remnants**.
