# Hookwash

[[01 World/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Mawreef Chain|Mawreef Chain]]

**Hookwash** is a publicly recognized Town within [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/Mawreef Chain|Mawreef Chain]].
It is known for salvage yards and hard-won hospitality.

Magic access is described publicly as **abundant near ports; practical enchantment follows fishing, salvage, and water infrastructure**.
