# Crownscar Interior

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Continents/Western and Eastern Continents#Prominence|Prominence]]

**Crownscar Interior** is a publicly recognized Broken upland corridor within [[01 World/Continents/Western and Eastern Continents#Prominence|Prominence]].
It is known for ruined capitals, oathstones, and fragile neutral ground.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **39000**.
Magic access is described publicly as **unstable and scarce; magic is feared, weaponized, or warped by occult forces**.
The local resource profile is **war-rationed supplies, salvage, and contaminated remnants**.
