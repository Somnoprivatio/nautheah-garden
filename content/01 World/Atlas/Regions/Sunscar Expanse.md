# Sunscar Expanse

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Continents/Western and Eastern Continents#Whoasis|Whoasis]]

**Sunscar Expanse** is a publicly recognized Desert-oasis corridor within [[01 World/Continents/Western and Eastern Continents#Whoasis|Whoasis]].
It is known for water rights, caravan diplomacy, and mirrored salt flats.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **42000**.
Magic access is described publicly as **very limited; practical low-level magic is guarded around cisterns**.
The local resource profile is **water and finished goods are rationed and convoy-controlled**.

Specific officials, private faction structures, and adventure hooks are kept out of this public summary.
