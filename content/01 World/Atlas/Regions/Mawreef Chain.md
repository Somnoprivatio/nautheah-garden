# Mawreef Chain

[[01 World/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Fisherman's Detriment|Fisherman's Detriment]]

**Mawreef Chain** is a publicly recognized Reef-island chain within [[01 World/Fisherman's Detriment|Fisherman's Detriment]].
It is known for deep salvage, tide charts, and dangerous creature lore.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **31000**.
Magic access is described publicly as **abundant near ports; practical enchantment follows fishing, salvage, and water infrastructure**.
The local resource profile is **rich in marine materials and salvage; manufactured luxuries vary by island**.

Specific officials, private faction structures, and adventure hooks are kept out of this public summary.
