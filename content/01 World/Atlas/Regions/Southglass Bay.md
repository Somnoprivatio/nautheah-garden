**Southglass Bay** is a publicly recognized Southern coastal trade region within [[01 World/Nations/Fali-City/Fali-City and New Anglia#Fali-City|Fali-City and New Anglia]].
It is known for merchant fleets, colored glass, and civic watchfulness.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **51000**.
Magic access is described publicly as **abundant; arcane services, reagents, and enchanted goods are common**.
The local resource profile is **well supplied by roads, workshops, and layered trade**.
