# Ash Banner Lowlands

[[01 World/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Western and Eastern Continents#Prominence|Prominence]]

**Ash Banner Lowlands** is a publicly recognized War-scarred agricultural plain within [[01 World/Western and Eastern Continents#Prominence|Prominence]].
It is known for refugee farms, memorial banners, and contested grain roads.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **61000**.
Magic access is described publicly as **unstable and scarce; magic is feared, weaponized, or warped by occult forces**.
The local resource profile is **war-rationed supplies, salvage, and contaminated remnants**.

Specific officials, private faction structures, and adventure hooks are kept out of this public summary.
