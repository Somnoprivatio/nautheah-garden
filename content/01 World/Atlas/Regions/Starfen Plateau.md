# Starfen Plateau

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Nations/Lakeland/Lakeland|Lakeland]]

**Starfen Plateau** is a publicly recognized High grassland and observatory district within [[01 World/Nations/Lakeland/Lakeland|Lakeland]].
It is known for astral study, wind farms, and sky-facing civic life.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **29000**.
Magic access is described publicly as **abundant around canals and cities; scholarly and industrial magic is widely traded**.
The local resource profile is **freshwater agriculture, barges, metalwork, and research goods**.

Specific officials, private faction structures, and adventure hooks are kept out of this public summary.
