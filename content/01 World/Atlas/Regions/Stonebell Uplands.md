# Stonebell Uplands

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Nations/Fali-City/Fali-City and New Anglia#Fali-City|Fali-City and New Anglia]]

**Stonebell Uplands** is a publicly recognized Mountain quarry district within [[01 World/Nations/Fali-City/Fali-City and New Anglia#Fali-City|Fali-City and New Anglia]].
It is known for quarry towns, resonant stone, and disciplined pilgrimage.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **33000**.
Magic access is described publicly as **abundant; arcane services, reagents, and enchanted goods are common**.
The local resource profile is **well supplied by roads, workshops, and layered trade**.

Specific officials, private faction structures, and adventure hooks are kept out of this public summary.
