# New Anglia

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]] · [[01 World/Nations/Fali-City/Fali-City and New Anglia|Legacy combined record]]

**New Anglia** is a publicly recognized nation. Its current public hierarchy includes the western settled coast, inland market country, and smaller mountain country; see the [[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index#New Anglia|Places Index]] for linked settlements and landmarks.

Specific officials, private faction structures, and adventure hooks are kept out of this public summary.

## Population and culture

New Anglia is primarily human, with established Aarakocra communities and many elves, halflings, dwarves, gnomes, and other trade-route peoples. Its ports and border towns are more diverse than its inland settlements.
