# Glasswind Coast

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] · [[01 World/Continents/Western and Eastern Continents#Whoasis|Whoasis]]

**Glasswind Coast** is a publicly recognized Tropical desert littoral within [[01 World/Continents/Western and Eastern Continents#Whoasis|Whoasis]].
It is known for salt, sea-glass, and cautious coastal trade.
Approximate population in the current gazetteer: **36000**.
Magic access is described publicly as **moderate near ports; new anglia trade brings occasional enchanted goods**.
The local resource profile is **sea-glass, coastal imports, and salt; inland routes remain constrained**.
