# Morning Strait

[[01 World/Atlas Index|Approved Locations Atlas]] · [[01 World/Places Index|Places Index]] · [[01 World/Fisherman's Detriment|Fisherman's Detriment]]

Morning Strait is the recognized water passage between [[01 World/Fisherman's Detriment#Morning Dew|Morning Dew]] and the mainland of Fisherman's Detriment. Its named approaches are [[01 World/Morning Strait#Dewbreak Anchorage|Dewbreak Anchorage]] and [[01 World/Morning Strait#Straitwatch Quay|Straitwatch Quay]].

## Public notes

- **Environment:** open strait, island approaches, and mainland coastal waters.
- **Role:** local passage between Morning Dew and the mainland.
- **Known approaches:** Dewbreak Anchorage and Straitwatch Quay.
