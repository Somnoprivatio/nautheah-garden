# Prominence

[[01 World/Atlas Index|Approved Locations Atlas]] · [[01 World/Places Index|Places Index]]

Prominence is a war-scarred eastern continent where magic is comparatively scarce, unreliable, or shaped by the damage left behind by conflict. Its roads and borders have changed repeatedly, so travelers are advised to confirm current conditions before crossing a region.

The continent contains old battlefields, hard-pressed settlements, and communities that have learned to treat arcane power with caution. The public atlas records its broad character without assigning every territory to a single authority.

## Public notes

- **Environment:** contested lowlands, damaged roads, and unsettled frontier country.
- **Travel:** conditions vary sharply by region.
- **Public reputation:** resilient, divided, and historically important.
