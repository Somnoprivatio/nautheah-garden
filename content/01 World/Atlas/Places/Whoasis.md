# Whoasis

[[01 World/Atlas Index|Approved Locations Atlas]] · [[01 World/Places Index|Places Index]]

Whoasis is a western continent of tropical coasts, a broad desert interior, and mountain country around a central oasis. Caravan roads and coastal routes connect settlements that may otherwise be separated by difficult terrain.

Its public reputation comes from long-distance trade, skilled guides, and the ability of local communities to move people and goods through harsh climates. Individual routes and regional politics are best confirmed locally.

## Public notes

- **Environment:** tropical coast, desert interior, and central mountain oasis.
- **Role:** caravan trade, coastal exchange, and overland travel.
- **Travel:** water, guides, and reliable route information are essential.
