# Astralythia

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Atlas Index|Approved Locations Atlas]] · [[01 World/Nations/Lakeland/Lakeland|Lakeland]]

Astralythia is a notable settlement within the territory of [[01 World/Nations/Lakeland/Lakeland|Lakeland]]. It is associated with celestial study, refined magic, and communities that look to the sky for guidance, timing, and omens.

Visitors commonly describe Astralythia as an orderly place where scholarly work and public life overlap. The public record does not claim that every local tradition or institution is understood outside the settlement.

## Public notes

- **Environment:** Lakeland territory with clear-sky and observatory traditions.
- **Role:** scholarly, arcane, and civic center.
- **Known connection:** Lakeland.
