# Confluence

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Atlas Index|Approved Locations Atlas]] · [[01 World/Atlas/Places/Simic Isles|Simic Isles]]

Confluence is a recognized settlement beneath the Simic Isles. It is associated with underwater travel, reef communities, and the meeting of several maritime and biological traditions.

Travelers should treat local access, customs, and routes as matters for local authorities. The public atlas records Confluence as an approved place without publishing private institutions or restricted history.

## Public notes

- **Environment:** underwater settlement among reefs and volcanic island waters.
- **Role:** maritime exchange, specialist craft, and cultural meeting place.
- **Known connection:** Simic Isles.
