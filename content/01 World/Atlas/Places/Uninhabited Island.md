# Uninhabited Island

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Atlas Index|Approved Locations Atlas]] · [[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

Uninhabited Island is a large island with no known government or permanent inhabitants. It has not been fully explored, so “uninhabited” remains a statement about current knowledge rather than proof that no life has ever lived there.

Coastal reports describe difficult landings and large stretches that remain absent from reliable charts. Any expedition should plan for incomplete information.

## Public notes

- **Environment:** large, partially charted island.
- **Status:** no known government or permanent settlement.
- **Travel:** exploration is incomplete and potentially hazardous.
