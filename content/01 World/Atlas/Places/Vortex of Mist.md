# Vortex of Mist

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Atlas Index|Approved Locations Atlas]] · [[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

The Vortex of Mist is a vast region of mana storms and extreme magical fluctuations. It is not a swamp; its defining hazards are unstable weather, distorted magic, and visibility that can change without warning.

The public record does not establish a single cause for the phenomenon. Sailors and scholars agree that ordinary navigation and spellwork should be treated cautiously within its influence.

## Population and culture

The Vortex is most strongly associated with aberrations and monstrosities altered by mana storms, planar pressure, or magical mutation. Stable settlements are rare and poorly documented; ordinary crews and stranded travelers may be encountered, but no conventional humanoid majority is publicly established.

## Public notes

- **Environment:** mana-storm region with persistent magical turbulence.
- **Travel:** dangerous for ships, spellcasters, and instruments.
- **Cause:** not publicly settled.
