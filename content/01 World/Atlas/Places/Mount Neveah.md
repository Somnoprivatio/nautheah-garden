# Mount Neveah

[[01 World/Atlas Index|Approved Locations Atlas]] · [[01 World/Places Index|Places Index]]

Mount Neveah is a high, rugged mountain region north of [[01 World/Atlas/Places/Prominence|Prominence]]. Snow-fed rivers descend from its passes, and isolated communities are connected by difficult trails, river routes, and highland trade.

The region is known for dramatic elevation changes, floating or cliff-bound terrain, and weather that can shift quickly. [[01 World/Atlas/Places/Cloudfall Harbor|Cloudfall Harbor]] is one of its best-known ports and approaches.

## Public notes

- **Environment:** alpine mountains, river valleys, and high passes.
- **Travel:** demanding; local guides and weather knowledge are valuable.
- **Known connection:** Cloudfall Harbor.
