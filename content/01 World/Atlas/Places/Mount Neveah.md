# Mount Neveah

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Atlas Index|Approved Locations Atlas]] · [[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

Mount Neveah is a high, rugged mountain region north of [[01 World/Atlas/Places/Prominence|Prominence]]. Snow-fed rivers descend from its passes, and isolated communities are connected by difficult trails, river routes, and highland trade.

The region is known for dramatic elevation changes, floating or cliff-bound terrain, and weather that can shift quickly. [[01 World/Atlas/Places/Cloudfall Harbor|Cloudfall Harbor]] is one of its best-known ports and approaches.

## Population and culture

Mount Neveah is a magical melting pot with strong communities of humans, fairies and other fey, shadar-kai, shifters, changelings, and kalashtar, alongside ordinary mountain peoples and visiting travelers. Magical creatures are common enough to shape local law, trade, pilgrimage, and folklore.

## Public notes

- **Environment:** alpine mountains, river valleys, and high passes.
- **Travel:** demanding; local guides and weather knowledge are valuable.
- **Known connections:** [[01 World/Atlas/Places/Cloudfall Harbor|Cloudfall Harbor]]; [[01 World/Prominent Regions/Mount Neveah/Thunderrock/Thunderrock|Thunderrock]]; the [[01 World/Prominent Regions/Mount Neveah/Swaying Peaks Brotherhood/Floating Home Temple|Swaying Peaks Brotherhood Floating Home Temple]].

## Highland communities and sacred sites

- **Thunderrock:** a volcanic, steamcraft-oriented settlement with a distinctive monastic tradition. Some local names use the form “of Thunderrock.”
- **Swaying Peaks Brotherhood:** a Drunken Fist monastic order whose floating home temple is associated with the region's shifting peaks.
- **Raven Queen home temple:** a mountain temple associated with local death, memory, and mourning rites; its formal name is not recorded in the public atlas.
- **Temple of Kelemvor and Church Garden:** a volcanic summit church and cultivated garden associated with Thunderrock. Detailed priestly and family history remains outside this public summary.
