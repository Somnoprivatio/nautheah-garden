# Selanora's Island

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Atlas Index|Approved Locations Atlas]] · [[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

Selanora's Island is a forested island realm associated with lunar tradition, celestial observation, and old maritime routes. Its surrounding waters and interior paths are described as beautiful but difficult to navigate without local knowledge.

Public accounts emphasize the island's cultural and spiritual importance while leaving its restricted sites and deeper history to local custodians.

## Public notes

- **Environment:** forested island and surrounding sea lanes.
- **Role:** lunar tradition, pilgrimage, and maritime landmark.
- **Travel:** local guidance is recommended.
