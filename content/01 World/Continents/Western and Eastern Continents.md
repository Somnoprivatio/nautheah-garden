# Western and Eastern Continents

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

## Whoasis

Whoasis is the western continent-scale political region. Tropical beaches give way to a broad desert interior, and mountains surround a central oasis. Water access, oasis trade, caravans, and mercenary work have all shaped its public reputation. The map uses the variant **Whoahasis**; **Whoasis** is the controlling vault spelling.

The public record does not establish a single continent-wide government. Recent allegations concerning [[05 Public Records/Current Public Warnings#Vorkharis|Vorkharis]] point toward threats connected to volcanic power, but the dragon's complete territorial system and internal command structure are not public knowledge.

## Prominence

Prominence is the eastern, war-torn continent. Its geography includes flatlands and tropical coast, while political authority remains divided and no single continental capital is established in the public register. Past commissions concerned both an earthquake-raised ruin and an underground market trafficking captives; those commissions are now recorded as completed.
