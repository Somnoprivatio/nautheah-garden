# Western and Eastern Continents

[[01 World/Places Index|Places Index]]

## Whoasis

Whoasis is the western continent. Tropical beaches give way to a broad desert interior, and mountains surround a central oasis. Water access, oasis trade, caravans, and mercenary work have all shaped its public reputation.

The public record does not establish a single continent-wide government. Recent allegations concerning [[05 Public Records/Current Public Warnings#Vorkharis|Vorkharis]] point toward threats connected to volcanic power, but the dragon's complete territorial system and internal command structure are not public knowledge.

## Prominence

Prominence is the eastern, war-torn continent. Its geography includes flatlands and tropical coast, while political authority remains divided. Past commissions concerned both an earthquake-raised ruin and an underground market trafficking captives; those commissions are now recorded as completed.
