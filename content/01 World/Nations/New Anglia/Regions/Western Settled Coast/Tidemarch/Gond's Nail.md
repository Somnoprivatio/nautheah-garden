# Gond's Nail

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

Gond's Nail is an open-sided Tidemarch shipwright shrine to Gond, built around a massive iron keel bolt. Craftspeople use it for offerings, trade notices, hiring and informal judgments of workmanship. Names of missing craftspeople have been tied to the shrine rail with copper wire. [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Nix Copperthumb|Nix Copperthumb]], the rescued goblin tidewright, left the Drowning Promise to seek work there after the crew returned to town.

Its connection to Nix is established in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 012 - Tidemarch Homecoming|Session 012]]. A detailed account of its staff and operations has not been received by the party.
