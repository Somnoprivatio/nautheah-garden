# Abe's Laboratory

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

The crew found [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - PC Backstory/Abe|Abe's]] concealed laboratory near Tidemarch, with altered creatures and Father Jack alive in a vat. Abe identified himself as Vesper's paternal grandfather. The battle continued through his Broken Seraph phase until Yama killed him.

Zaphkiel the Watcher appeared after the victory and removed Abe's remains. Father Jack died after being transformed into a flesh golem; Vesper also died during the confrontation.

On departure, Xavier ignited the roots above the site. The great tree collapsed into the laboratory after the party got clear. **The laboratory is destroyed.** Specific uncollected stock and final divisions of recovered objects remain outside the shared inventory.
