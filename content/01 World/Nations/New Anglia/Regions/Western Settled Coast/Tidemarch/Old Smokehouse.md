# Old Smokehouse

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

The Old Smokehouse occupies the former site of Vesper's mother's general-goods shop in Tidemarch. It now combines a smokehouse, smoking lounge and performance venue under [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Veylan Giltbrush|Veylan Giltbrush]], the two-tailed vulpin impresario.

Jinx gave a flaming-knife and circus-ball performance here and negotiated a ten-percent venue arrangement. Veylan later praised his talent before the crew left town.

**Shared visit:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 012 - Tidemarch Homecoming|Session 012]]; departure reference in [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 015 - Vesper's Farewell and Lakeland Arrival|Session 015]].
