# The Nine Bells

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]] · [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch|Tidemarch]] · [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch/Nine Bells Quay|Nine Bells Quay]] · [[02 People/Hesta Pike|Hesta Pike]]

The Nine Bells is a dockside restaurant, tavern, and inn attached to Nine Bells Quay. It serves filling harbor meals, houses sailors and travelers, and provides a private back room for captains and local families.

Nine salvaged ship bells hang beneath the eaves, each with a different tone. They announce shift changes, tide notices, arrivals, and storm warnings. The inn is owned and operated by [[02 People/Hesta Pike|Hesta Pike]].
