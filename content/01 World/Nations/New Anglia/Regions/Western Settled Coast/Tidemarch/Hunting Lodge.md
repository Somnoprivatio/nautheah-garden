# Hunting Lodge

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

On the return from Abe's laboratory, the party inspected an unlocked hunting lodge and its garden. The resident was absent, and the crew chose to leave without waiting.

The owner's identity and relationship to the surrounding events have not been established.
