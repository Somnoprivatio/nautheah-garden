# Nine Bells Quay

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]] · [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch|Tidemarch]] · [[01 World/Nations/New Anglia/Regions/Western Settled Coast/Tidemarch/The Nine Bells|The Nine Bells]] · [[02 People/Tobren Vale|Tobren Vale]]

Nine Bells Quay is a quieter berth at the outer edge of Tidemarch's Low Quays. The public harbor uses it for private, overflow, repair, quarantine, and controlled dockings that should not obstruct the main commercial waterfront.

The quay has deep-water access at favorable tides, stout mooring points, a service crane, and a covered walk connecting it directly to The Nine Bells. Quay Dockmaster [[02 People/Tobren Vale|Tobren Vale]] assigns its berths and directs incoming vessels by signal flag.
