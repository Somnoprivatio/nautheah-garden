# Lion's Pride Inn

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

The Lion's Pride Inn is a Forever Port inn with an attached working blacksmith. [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Dorrin Flintmantle|Dorrin Flintmantle]], the dwarf rescued from the wagon, works as its blacksmith after his release.

The inn's proprietor and exact district have not been established.
