# Talontinous Delight

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

Talontinous Delight is [[02 People/World/NPC - Shop/Keelwhisper|Keelwhisper's]] tavern and protected meeting place for smugglers exchanging goods and services in Forever Port.

## What the crew was shown

An apparent crowd masks an otherwise empty contact venue for people who complete the recognition exchange. Enchanted instruments play without musicians. Wren described her enhanced unseen-servant work and the false-crowd illusion.

The menus sense what each diner wants; unseen servants prepare individualized meals. A meal grants the diner a d10 Bardic Inspiration that lasts until used. Wren's concealed workshop is reached through the cooler by pulling a frozen lamb leg, and her four mechanical crows watch outside.

The crew delivered Maclear's delayed cargo here, reunited with Wren and received testimony about the seeds, unusual currents and the Cinder Ledger handoff. Keelwhisper said the cargo payment would be waiting aboard the ship.

## Recognition exchange received by the crew

**Challenge:** "The waves whisper beneath the waves."

**Response:** "The whispers request the taste of Selanora's Delights."

These details are knowledge disclosed to the crew, rather than an ordinary public tavern directory.
