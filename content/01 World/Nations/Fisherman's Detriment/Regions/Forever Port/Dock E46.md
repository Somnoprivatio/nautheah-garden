# Dock E46

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

Dock E46 is the Forever Port berth named in the Cinder Ledger handoff lead. Keelwhisper expected the vessel near **2:00 AM on 9 Sunglade, Year 513**, within a one-to-three-hour arrival window, with departure before noon after paperwork and bribes.

The party later delayed the ship by damaging its rudder and confirmed captives below deck. The completed rescue and destruction occurred later at sea; this historical berth lead is not the vessel's present location.

**Shared discovery:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 005 - Captive Wagon and Cinder Ledger|Session 005]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 006 - Infiltrating the Cinder Ledger|Session 006]].
