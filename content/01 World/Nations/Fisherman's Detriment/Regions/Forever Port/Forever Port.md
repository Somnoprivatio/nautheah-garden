# Forever Port

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]] · [[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Fisherman's Detriment|Fisherman's Detriment]] · [[03 Factions/Authorities/Iron Authority|Iron Authority]] · [[05 Public Records/Forever Port Gazette|Forever Port Gazette]]

Forever Port is a major harbor city within **Fisherman's Detriment**. It never closes: lighthouses remain lit, vessels move through the harbor at all hours, and current records describe it as the world's busiest center of commerce.

## Publicly established locations and institutions

### Harbor and lighthouse ring

The harbor remains active day and night. The **Iron Authority**, called the Fisherman's Detriment Port Authority in regional records, patrols the surrounding waters and performs visible security, inspection, and port-guard functions.

The [[03 Factions/Authorities/Iron Authority|Iron Authority]] is Forever Port's naval military. It operates under [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Vorkesh Sorn|Vorkesh Sorn]] and provides the city's dedicated naval defense and maritime enforcement.

### Talontinous Delight

[[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Regions/Forever Port/Forever Port#Talontinous Delight|Talontinous Delight]] is a tavern and restaurant operated by [[02 People/World/NPC - Shop/Keelwhisper|Keelwhisper]], titled **Whisper beneath the Keel**. It is a known meeting place for maritime contacts and the location where the Drowning Promise arranged delivery of Maclear's delayed cargo.

### Forever Port Gazette

The [[05 Public Records/Forever Port Gazette|Forever Port Gazette]] circulates through Forever Port and along Nautheah's shipping routes. Its reports are an important public source for harbor warnings, missing-person reports, and New Anglia's Vorkharis allegations.

### Drowning Promise berth

The **Drowning Promise** reached Forever Port after a three-day voyage from Elpa. Its crew's public campaign record places the ship in the city during the Talontinous Delight contact and the subsequent investigation.

## What remains unknown

No public district map, permanent population count, complete government hierarchy, or comprehensive list of businesses has been established. The city's active harbor is known; its full internal geography is not.
