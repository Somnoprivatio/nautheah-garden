# Drow Moon Temple

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

This Forever Port temple serves [[04 Lore/Deities/Active/Selune|Selune]], the lesser moon goddess specifically associated with drow worship in Nautheah. [[02 People/Campaign Specific/NPC - Story/Ilivara Myrynda|High Priestess Grace, Ilivara Myrynda]], is the known priestess.

The party brought Mara Fenlock here after the wagon rescue. The temple cared for her dehydration and confinement, while Dorrin Flintmantle chose to remain until she was safe.

The page title describes the temple encountered by the crew; a formal institutional name was not supplied.

**Shared visit:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 005 - Captive Wagon and Cinder Ledger|Session 005]].
