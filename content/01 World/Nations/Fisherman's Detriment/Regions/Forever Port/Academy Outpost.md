# Academy Outpost

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

The [[03 Factions/Orders and Schools/Fali-City Adventuring Academy|Fali-City Adventuring Academy]] has an outpost in Forever Port through which messages and meetings can be arranged.

The crew used the network to contact Aeris Moonshadow and then met academy contacts Nana Mori and Malithar Windrider. Aeris offered conditional access to the Circle of the Moon. Academy magic returned the party to the Drowning Promise.

**Shared visits:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 005 - Captive Wagon and Cinder Ledger|Session 005]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 006 - Infiltrating the Cinder Ledger|Session 006]].
