# Elpa

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

**Elpa, City of the Two Tides**, is a major maritime city within Fisherman's Detriment, built above and beneath a sheltered bay. Circular districts, canals, aqueducts and harbor works carry water through its surface and submerged neighborhoods. Dry tunnels, flooded passages and transition chambers accommodate land-dwelling, aquatic and amphibious visitors.

It began as a Triton harbor settlement and grew with Simic Hybrid refugees, artisans and engineers. Triton stonework and Simic approaches to adaptation shaped the city's expanding rings. Azul's whirlpool pendants and an accessible altar were visible during the party's visit. Sea powers are widely honored; the city has no single established civic orthodoxy.

## Civic life and shops

Leroy Jenk serves as a captain in the main port guard and has a courthouse office. House Jenk and House Shellstitch are important public family names. Matt Square connects the harbor to a multilevel surface mall.

- [[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Regions/Elpa/Shops/Keelbreaker Blades|Keelbreaker Blades]]: Caldris Vane's above-water maritime blade shop.
- [[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Regions/Elpa/Shops/The Brined Crucible|The Brined Crucible]]: Ilyon Marshglass's dry underground reagent shop.
- [[01 World/Nations/Fisherman's Detriment/Regions/Elpa/Shops/The Second Skin|The Second Skin]]: Veyra Shellstitch's tailor, with air-side and underwater fitting access.

## Crew's visits

The Northern Current Carnies provisioned, commissioned weapons, stole Captain's Pride armor and escaped a guard pursuit. The Second Skin was later ransacked by an unidentified party. The crew collected the commissioned weapons and Jinx's finished clothes before departure. Leroy's later payment and favor were settled on the return visit.

Roughly 150,000 people were present during the first visit; that observation is not a permanent-resident census.
