# The Second Skin

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

The Second Skin is [[02 People/World/NPC - Shop/Veyra Shellstitch|Veyra Shellstitch's]] tailoring business in the multilevel surface mall at Elpa's Matt Square. It has both air-side and underwater fitting access.

Jinx was measured for fine clothes. He later used Sleep on Veyra while Trifoot stole Captain's Pride armor, prompting the guard pursuit. The shop was subsequently emptied and ransacked by an unidentified party. Elpa guards reported Veyra's recovery and identified Oil of Slipperiness on the thieves' route.

Veyra completed Jinx's clothing from personal stock; the stolen stock prevented completion of Ryskulla's armor commission. The later ransacking remains an open mystery.
