# Keelbreaker Blades

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

Keelbreaker Blades is [[02 People/World/NPC - Shop/Caldris Vane|Caldris Vane's]] above-water sword shop near Elpa's quays. It makes and maintains maritime blades, sells swords, daggers and practical bladed tools, and uses rear pressure-testing tanks for underwater work.

The party commissioned Ryskulla's light-hammer enchantment and Trifoot's enhanced warning scimitar here, collecting both before departure. Jinx acquired the Inverse Rapier of Verdict, and Xavier obtained a jeweled dagger for use as a costly spell component.

**Shared visits:** [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 002 - Elpa Heist, Enchantments, and a Curse|Session 002]] and [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Sessions/Session 003 - A Charlatan's Bargain and Forever Port|Session 003]].
