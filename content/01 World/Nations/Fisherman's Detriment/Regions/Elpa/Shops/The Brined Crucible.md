# The Brined Crucible

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

The Brined Crucible is [[02 People/World/NPC - Shop/Ilyon Marshglass|Ilyon Marshglass's]] reagent and alchemical-supply shop in a dry underground commercial area of Elpa, close to a walkable water-transition ring.

The crew bought measured reagents and component pouches here. Ilyon gave Jinx a sigil-covered stone intended for Ryskulla.
