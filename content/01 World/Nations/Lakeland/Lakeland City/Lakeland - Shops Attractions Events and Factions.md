# Lakeland: Shops, Attractions, Events and Factions

[[01 World/Nations/Lakeland/Lakeland City/Lakeland City|Lakeland City]] · [[01 World/Nations/Lakeland/Lakeland Index|Lakeland]] · [[03 Factions/Faction Index|Faction Index]]

Lakeland is a vast, wealthy canal capital where foreign freight, farm carts, enchanted goods and street performances share the waterfront. Its residents come from many ancestries, and its services range from practical workshops to rare magical retreats.

## Shops, food and lodging

| Shop or venue | Goods and services | Character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Varrick Coinbeard's Shop** | Magical goods, scrolls, trinkets and advice on materials for particular crafted items | A small entrance opens into an enormous desert-bazaar environment. |
| **Reed and Iron** | Weapons, shields, armor fitting, work tools and basic equipment | A practical smith's shop with displays and fittings for customers of different sizes. |
| **The Sealed Leaf** | Books, inks, scroll cases, copying, translation and seal comparison | A specialist stop for readers, scribes and people handling formal documents. |

| **Blue Thread Atelier** | Travel clothes, formalwear, costume alterations and stage masks | Tailoring for everyday needs, performances and important occasions. |
| **Anchor and Atlas** | Charts, regional maps, surveying gear and route advice | A useful stop before traveling unfamiliar waterways or roads. |
| **Farshore Exchange** | Foreign coin, freight valuation, trade-credit introductions and mineral-assay referrals | Commercial services for travelers and merchants. |
| **Glasswake Curios** | Lenses, spectacles, glassware, small clockwork curios and novelty enchantments | A browsing shop with unusual demonstrations and ingenious little devices. |
| **Sunfield Covered Market** | Produce, smoked fish, rice dishes, spices, street food and provisions | A busy meeting point between the surrounding farms and the city. |
| **The Three Lanterns** | Inn rooms, hot meals, private tables and evening music | Comfortable lodging with space to meet over a meal. |

Stock, prices, commissions and room availability are confirmed with each business.

| **The Pearl Veil** | Luxury bathhouse and courtesan salon | Mineral pools, perfumed steam, massage, trained courtesan hosts, live music, private bathing pavilions and elegant companionship by appointment. |
| **The Saffron Crescent** | Hammam and thermal spa | Warm stone chambers, scented scrubs, hot and cool pools, tea courtyards and communal or private treatments. |

| **The Gilded Lily** | Grooming and perfumery | Hair and beard styling, cosmetics, custom scents, horn care, scale polishing and services for many ancestries. |
| **The Velvet Wake** | Cabaret and supper club | Burlesque, dance, comedy, live ensembles, late dining, private boxes and visiting performers. |
| **The Stillglass Rooms** | Magical sensory retreats | Private chambers styled as gardens, seashores or star-filled landscapes, with adjustable sound, light and temperature. |
| **The Thousandfold Table** | Culinary theatre | Tasting menus, rare regional cooking, illusion-accompanied courses and private chef's tables. |
| **The Hushfold Suites** | Discreet luxury lodging | Quiet suites, private dining, personal valets, correspondence service, meeting rooms and a concierge. |
| **The Lacquered Hand** | Games and social club | Cards, strategy games, billiards, private tournaments, a reading lounge and a supper room. |
| **Jade Wake Gondolas** | Private canal excursions | Covered pleasure boats, candlelit suppers, optional musicians, floral decorations and scenic evening routes. |

Private pavilions, hosted evenings, club events and specialist treatments may require an appointment or invitation.

## Attractions and places to spend time

| Place or activity | What visitors can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Library of Lake** | Read, consult maps and pursue research. Ask about access to particular collections. |
| **Church of Light and Knowledge** | Visit Oghma's church, attend public teaching or seek scholarly and scribal assistance. |
| **Azul's edge-of-town temple** | Visit the smaller temple set away from the busiest streets, where followers gather outside. |
| **The water gardens** | Walk beside Eldath's pools, watch canal birds or find a quiet place to talk. |
| **Movable landing overlook** | Watch the defensive landings open and waterborne traffic pass through. |
| **Castle-facing public quays** | Enjoy views of the central castle and the waterfront from public streets. |
| **Canal performance walks** | Find musicians, jugglers, dancers and illusionists along the canals. |
| **Skyship spotting** | Watch the skies for traffic on the Falicity-Lakeland route, including the Eagle-class **Cutting Air 1**. Check locally for movements and viewing opportunities. |
