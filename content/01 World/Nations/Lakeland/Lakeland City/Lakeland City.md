# Lakeland City

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

Lakeland City is the capital of the nation and continent of Lakeland, connected to fertile farmland, a great lake and extensive waterways. It is a large, diverse center of trade, government and learning. Its known civic institutions include the **Library of Lake** and **Church of Light and Knowledge**, devoted to Oghma.

## Arrival and travel

The Drowning Promise arrived on **14 Harvestshade, Year 513**, after a twelve-day voyage from Tidemarch. Entry required three hours of verification. Controlled currents guided the ship to its assigned internal dock, and officials supplied local laws.
