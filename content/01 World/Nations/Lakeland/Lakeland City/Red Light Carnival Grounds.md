# Red Light Carnival Grounds

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

Lakeland's advertisements direct visitors beyond the city road toward red lights and a large red tent. The advertised show is **A lovely little terrible joke**, scheduled for the evening of **14 Harvestshade, Year 513**.

The crew has received the invitation through the public advertising. A visit, performance and on-site roster have not yet been recorded as shared events.
