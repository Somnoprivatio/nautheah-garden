# Varrick Coinbeard's Shop

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

Varrick Coinbeard operates this magical-goods business in Lakeland City. A small entrance opens onto a much larger desert-bazaar environment. The shop offers magical stock and components specific to the item a customer intends to craft.

The title is a descriptive reference to Varrick's business; a formal shop name has not been supplied.
