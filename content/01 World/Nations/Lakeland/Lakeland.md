# Lakeland

[[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

Lakeland is a nation and continent organized around fertile country, lakes, and navigable waterways. Its capital, **Lakeland City**, is also the continent's capital.

## Population and culture

Lakeland is a coastal melting pot. Humans, elves, dwarves, halflings, gnomes, dragonborn, aquatic peoples, and many other ancestries move through its ports, canals, lake cities, and trade roads. Lakeland City and the major waterways are especially diverse, while rural lake communities retain more localized traditions.

## Lakeland City

Lakeland City is a major center of government, learning, agriculture, and river-and-lake travel. Publicly known institutions include the Library of Lake and the Church of Light and Knowledge, associated with Oghma. The surrounding countryside contains productive farmland and engineered defenses.

Past public commissions in and around the capital include the recovery of a missing treasure caravan, stopping a rogue iron construct, and laying a library ghost to rest. See [[05 Public Records/Completed Commissions|Completed Commissions]].

## Astralythia

Astralythia is a recognized territory within the nation and continent of Lakeland. Its exact borders, government, settlements, and cultural institutions have not yet been established in the public record.
