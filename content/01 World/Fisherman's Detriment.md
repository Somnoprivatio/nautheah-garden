# Fisherman's Detriment

[[01 World/Places Index|Places Index]]

Fisherman's Detriment is a tropical, mountainous island nation and archipelago. Dangerous creatures, waterways, irregular coastlines, fishing, shipping, and informal trade all shape life there. The [[03 Factions/Fisherman's Detriment Port Authority|Port Authority]] maintains a visible maritime presence.

## Elpa, City of the Two Tides

Elpa is a city built both above and beneath the water. Triton origins and later Simic refugee engineering helped create a settlement that can serve terrestrial and aquatic residents. Its public shops include:

- **The Second Skin**, adaptive tailoring by [[02 People/Merchants and Proprietors#Veyra Shellstitch|Veyra Shellstitch]].
- **Keelbreaker Blades**, maritime swordsmithing by [[02 People/Merchants and Proprietors#Caldris Vane|Caldris Vane]].
- **The Brined Crucible**, reagents and alchemical supplies by [[02 People/Merchants and Proprietors#Ilyon Marshglass|Ilyon Marshglass]].

## Morning Dew

Morning Dew is a settlement within Fisherman's Detriment. Farmers and sailors share a community shaped by the sea and devotion to [[04 Lore/Faiths of Nautheah#Azul|Azul]]. The exact population and civic structure are not yet part of the public register.

## Forever Port

Forever Port is a harbor city that never closes. Its lighthouses remain lit and ships move through its waters at all hours. It is described in current records as the world's busiest center of commerce. The port is home to Talontinous Delight, operated by [[02 People/Merchants and Proprietors#Keelwhisper|Keelwhisper]], and to the widely circulating [[05 Public Records/Forever Port Gazette|Forever Port Gazette]].
