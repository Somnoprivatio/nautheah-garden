# Swaying Peaks Brotherhood Floating Home Temple

The Swaying Peaks Brotherhood maintains a floating home temple among Mount Neveah's shifting peaks. It is associated with Drunken Fist training, communal discipline, and the mountain's constantly changing routes.

The temple's exact route and anchoring method are not published. Travelers generally rely on Brotherhood guides or local highland knowledge to find it.
