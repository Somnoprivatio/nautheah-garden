# Swaying Peaks Brotherhood Floating Home Temple

The Swaying Peaks Brotherhood maintains a floating home temple among Mount Neveah's shifting peaks. It is associated with Drunken Fist training, communal discipline, and the mountain's constantly changing routes.

The temple's exact route and anchoring method are not published. Travelers generally rely on Brotherhood guides or local highland knowledge to find it.

**Tags:** #place #monastery #public-record

## Connection to the crew

The [[03 Factions/Orders and Schools/Swaying Peaks Brotherhood|Swaying Peaks Brotherhood]] honors Hanseath through fellowship, Drunken Fist discipline, brewing and the preservation of truthful history. Its librarian Galdric Tombstone died in the final revelry he shared with the Drowning Promise's crew. His [[06 Campaign Lore/Northern Current Carnies/Galdric Tombstone Materials/Galdric Tombstone — Journal and History of the Swaying Peaks Brotherhood|recovered journal and history]] describe the order and its older journeys.

The crew has received those writings but has not visited the floating temple.
