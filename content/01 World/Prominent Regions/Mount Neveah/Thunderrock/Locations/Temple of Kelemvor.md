# Temple of Kelemvor and Church Garden

Atop Thunderrock's volcanic heights stands a church surrounded by a cultivated garden. The site is known for its moonlit atmosphere, steam vents, and solemn rites concerning death and remembrance.

Public records identify the church with **Kelemvor**. The priestess, family history, and the garden's unusual blue moonlit tree are retained in campaign records rather than this high-level public summary.
