# Thunderrock

[[01 World/Prominent Regions/Mount Neveah/Mount Neveah Index|Mount Neveah]] · [[01 World/00 Indexes/Places Index|Places Index]]

Thunderrock is a volcanic highland settlement known for steamcraft, mining, and a local monastic tradition. Some residents use the form **“of Thunderrock”** in place of a family surname.

The settlement's summit church and garden, monastic order, and workshop districts are recognized features of Mount Neveah, although detailed local history is not part of the public atlas yet.
