# Raven Queen Home Temple

Mount Neveah is home to a mountain temple associated with Raven Queen traditions of memory, mourning, and the passage from life to death. It is connected to the early history of Vaelith Karanthar, though the temple's formal name and exact settlement are not published in the public atlas.
