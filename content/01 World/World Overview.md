# World Overview

[[00 Home/Start Here|Home]] · [[01 World/Places Index|Places]]

Nautheah's known geography joins large continental powers, island nations, mountain regions, unsettled expanses, and seas shaped by dependable long-distance currents.

| Area | Public overview |
| --- | --- |

| [[01 World/Fali-City and New Anglia#New Anglia\|New Anglia]] | Agricultural and trade territory influenced by Fali-City, marked by volcanic mountains and decentralized settlements. |
| [[01 World/Lakeland\|Lakeland]] | Nation and continent whose capital is Lakeland City. |

| [[01 World/Great Regions and Islands\|Great regions and islands]] | Mount Neveah, the Simic Isles, Selanora's Island, Suckstuck, the Vortex of Mist, and the Uninhabited Island. |

The known oceans are crossed by the [[04 Lore/Ocean Currents|Northern and Southern Currents]], which shape travel, trade, and isolation.
