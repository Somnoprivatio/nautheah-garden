# World Overview

[[00 Home/Start Here|Home]] · [[01 World/Places Index|Places]]

Nautheah's known geography joins large continental powers, island nations, mountain regions, unsettled expanses, and seas shaped by dependable long-distance currents.

The current world map is a geographic naming reference rather than a claim that every label is a nation. Political entries, regions, islands, and capitals are separated in the [[01 World/Places Index|Places Index]].

| Area | Public overview |
| --- | --- |
| [[01 World/Fali-City and New Anglia\|Fali-City]] | Nation and continental capital; a major center of trade, faith, learning, and adventuring. |
| [[01 World/Atlas/Regions/New Anglia\|New Anglia]] | Separate nation of volcanic mountains, agricultural and trade settlements, shipbuilding, and coastal labor. |
| [[01 World/Atlas/Places/Simic Isles\|Simic Isles]] | Volcanic island nation known for living alchemy, reef communities, and specialist biological craft. |
| [[01 World/Lakeland\|Lakeland]] | Nation and continent whose capital is Lakeland City. |

| [[01 World/Western and Eastern Continents#Whoasis\|Whoasis]] | Western continent of coasts, desert, mountains, oasis trade, and mercenary traditions. |
| [[01 World/Western and Eastern Continents#Prominence\|Prominence]] | Eastern, war-torn continent with divided authority and varied terrain. |
| [[01 World/Great Regions and Islands\|Prominent regions and islands]] | Mount Neveah, Selanora's Island, Suckstuck, the Vortex of Mist, and the Uninhabited Island. |

The known oceans are crossed by the [[04 Lore/Ocean Currents|Northern and Southern Currents]], which shape travel, trade, and isolation.
